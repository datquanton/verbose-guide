How conclusions are tested

The file's most reusable output is not a view on any company. It is a catalogue of the specific ways a number can be right and still mislead — each one found the hard way, most of them by catching the file's own errors.

Measure substitution
Two figures compared on different bases. The catalogue now runs to basis, instrument, date, unit, tenor, channel, window, sample, statistic and seniority. A recent example: a monthly mean rising while an intramonth minimum was set — both true of the same series, and neither refuting the other.
The gate table
Roughly ninety date-fenced rows recording what is already established and when it may next be searched. It exists because the same topics were re-researched ten times over. It is checked before a search, not after — and it has twice caught a re-derivation in progress.
Pre-registration
The chain from evidence to conclusion is written down before the data arrives, so its failure is measurable rather than quietly re-narrated — including when the failure is that the test was unfalsifiable by construction, because the instrument’s mechanism had been assumed rather than checked. It has already retired one of the file's own macro frameworks: a 42.6-point collapse in Federal Reserve hike odds moved the dollar index by four basis points, breaking the chain at its first link.
Right for the wrong reason is a failure
Scored that way in the calibration log. A correct direction reached through a broken mechanism is not banked as a win.
Robustness has axes
Varying one input across its full plausible range and calling a conclusion robust is a strictly weaker claim than it sounds. One derivation here was declared robust across the rebar-price axis and later withdrawn: a second, larger axis existed, and it reversed the sign.
Decomposition outranks a bare total
A total that survives being broken into named parts beats one that does not. It settled a flow figure that arrived in two irreconcilable renderings.
Bare numbers are the worst search term
They have no word boundary and live inside every longer number and percentage. Grepping 838 returned twenty-three hits, all of them 1.838% — and nearly suppressed a material finding rather than merely wasting time.
Source tiers, and partial adoption
Sources are rated T1 to T5 and are not adopted or rejected whole. One page's market capitalisation was used because it agreed with the model to 1.66%; the headline price on the same page was refused because it did not agree with its own capitalisation.
Unobservable is not the same as absent
A search that returns nothing is recorded as a blocked route with the host named, not as evidence that nothing happened. Roughly fifteen hosts are unreachable from this environment and each is listed rather than retried.

How this file is kept

An unattended run sweeps every hour. Three lanes are checked every time — Vietnamese macro and policy, United States trade action on Vietnamese steel, and company news across all eight names. Four more rotate: index and flows, sector reads, competitors and the street, and ownership signals. Each sweep is budgeted at four to eight targeted searches.

A sweep that finds something material appends a dated entry, escalates any model input that changed, re-runs both engines, tests the five triggers, scores any resolved forecast, and commits. A sweep that finds nothing material commits nothing and says which lanes it checked. That distinction is enforced, and roughly one sweep in eight ends without a commit.

What the automation deliberately cannot do. It cannot change a valuation input, retune a scenario branch, place or size a trade, or rewrite a gate row's contents. When it finds that an input is wrong it quantifies the consequence — including, where the arithmetic allows, the exact effect on expected return — and stops. Every escalation on this page reached it that way.
