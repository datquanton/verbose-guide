Coverage — 2026 theses in one line

KDH — Khang Dien property

  • Q1 FS read: +131% headline rests on a ₫285bn bargain-purchase gain; core PBT ≈ ₫70bn; debt +51% QoQ, interest fully capitalized (₫225bn/qtr)
  • Operationally on track: Gladia 60% sold at 65% gross margin, deposits consolidated & back-loaded to 12/2026; 2026 is a one-project year

TCB — Techcombank bank

  • Record Q2 (₫9.7tn PBT), NIM back to 3.4%, CASA 38.3%; 7% cash + 60% bonus shares pending
  • Model: even bull-case grid reaches only ~93% of the ₫37.5tn top target — guidance needs NIM recovery

VPB — VPBank bank

  • Record 1H (₫18.9tn PBT, +68%), credit +24.6% YTD vs 35% ceiling; 26% stock dividend + $250m foreign placement ahead
  • Model: the bank engine alone reaches 65–79% of the ₫41.6tn target — plan leans on VPBankS; FE Credit credit-cost is the swing factor

TCX — TCBS broker

  • Record Q2 (₫2.1tn PBT), largest margin book (₫51.5tn), 47% of FY plan at H1; VN30 entry Aug 3
  • Most expensive large broker (~2.4x book est.); FTSE Sep-21 event tree prices at ≈ +6% EV — a kicker, not a thesis

VPX — VPBankS broker

  • Sector-best Q2 growth (PBT 4x) but H1 leaned on ₫3.5tn FVTPL marks — core vs marks modeled separately
  • Cheapest broker growth (~1.4–1.5x book est.), zero sell-side coverage; CAEX crypto-license option ≈ +5% EV

HPG — Hoa Phat steel

  • Q1 IR read: 45% of record profit was the Pho Noi divestment gain; core +47% with DQ2 at 100% capacity
  • ~8x earnings, P/B at historical bottom zone; Jul-28 US rebar final is headline risk (~3% of revenue); watch the HRC spread
