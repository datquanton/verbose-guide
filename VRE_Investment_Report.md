## Investment thesis

We rate **VRE a BUY with a 12-month target price of VND34,972**, ~47% above the current VND23,800. The stock has de-rated to ~1.1x trailing P/B — close to its 2024 trough (0.9x) and ~40% below its eight-year average (~1.9x) — at the very moment its operating metrics are inflecting. 1Q2026 delivered leasing revenue +9.0% YoY, NPATMI +36.4%, system occupancy of 88.9% (+2.8ppt) and footfall +13.2%, on a balance sheet carrying net debt/equity of just 4.9%. Our target is set on a three-year FCFF DCF and corroborated by a P/B that remains below mid-cycle even after the move. **The core call is simple: the market is pricing VRE as a melting ice cube under e-commerce and Aeon pressure, while the leasing engine is quietly compounding and a Mega-Mall pipeline in Vinhomes townships is about to add ~11% to GFA.** The catalysts are timing-based and trackable, not existential.

Where we differ from the consensus narrative — and from the AI-assisted competitor initiation now circulating — is on **earnings quality**. We do not take the reported VND6,446bn 2025 PAT at face value, and we are explicit that a growing share of VRE's "profit" is interest income on related-party deposits rather than rent. We think that distinction *raises* conviction rather than lowering it, because it means the de-rating has been driven by headline noise that obscures a durable, high-margin leasing annuity.

## 1. 1Q2026: the operating inflection is real

VRE reported 1Q2026 total revenue of **VND2,294bn (+7.6% YoY)** and **NPATMI of VND1,606bn (+36.4%)**, completing 25% of full-year revenue guidance and ~30% of the profit plan in the seasonally soft first quarter. Stripping the divested Vincom Center Nguyễn Chí Thanh, like-for-like leasing revenue rose **+11.8%** and like-for-like NPATMI **+38.4%** — i.e., the underlying portfolio is accelerating, not coasting on disposals.

The quality indicators all moved the right way:
- **Occupancy 88.9%** (+2.8ppt YoY), led by Vincom Center (97.1%) and Vincom Mega Mall (92.7%), with provincial Vincom Plaza at 84.1% and Vincom+ recovering to 81.5%.
- **Footfall +13.2%** at malls (plus +15.9% at the new commercial-street format), with experience-led F&B and Entertainment categories taking ~52% of new leasing area — the right mix to defend against both e-commerce and Aeon's destination model.
- **Leasing NOI margin 70.4%** and EBITDA margin 62.6% — among the highest of any listed SEA mall operator.

Macro is a tailwind, not a headwind: 1Q26 GDP grew +7.83% (a 15-year high), real retail sales +7%, and FDI disbursement hit a record. New competing supply is modest (Hanoi +58,000 m², HCMC flat), and CBD asking rents posted single-digit growth — supportive of the rent step-ups embedded in VRE's renewals.

## 2. Earnings quality — read past the headline (our differentiator)

This is where a model earns its keep. VRE's 2025 reported PBT of ~VND8,083bn breaks into three very different streams that deserve three very different multiples:

| Profit stream | 2025 (VNDbn) | Character | Our treatment |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Core mall-leasing operating profit | ~3,893 | Recurring, defensive, high-margin annuity | Value at a premium multiple |
| Net financial income | ~3,687 | Interest on ~VND29.5tn of cash/deposits/**intercompany lending** (incl. Vingroup/VinFast affiliates at 10–13%) | Recurring run-rate ~VND1,900bn; haircut for related-party/transparency risk |
| One-off disposal gain (Vincom NCT) | ~1,900 | Non-recurring | Exclude from valuation earnings |

Two points follow. **First**, "clean" 2025 earnings are closer to **VND4.5–5.0tn**, not the reported VND6.4tn — so the optically cheap ~9.8x reported P/E is really ~14–15x on normalised EPS, and the *honest* cheapness is on **P/B**, where VRE sits near a multi-year trough despite double-digit ROE. **Second**, VRE's cash/deposits/intercompany-lending balance jumped from ~VND17tn (2024) to **~VND29.5tn (2025)**, including a fresh ~VND6.2tn deposit to a related party for "future mall investment," generating ~VND2.5tn of recurring interest income at ~9%. That income is real, but it is *financial*, not operational, and it is concentrated in the Vingroup ecosystem.

We treat this transparently: in our DCF we value the **leasing FCFF** as the durable engine and normalise financial income down to ~VND1,900bn from 2026 (as the one-offs roll off). The related-party concentration is the single most important item we want management to disclose more granularly — counterparties, tenors, rates, and security. **This is precisely the analysis an AI-generated report glosses over, and where Mirae adds value the screen cannot.**

## 3. Growth pipeline and a dated catalyst path

VRE is entering a new opening cycle: **~214,000 m² of GFA (~+11%)** of mostly Vincom Mega Malls tied to Vinhomes townships, where tens of thousands of captive residents compress the usual ramp-up. The pipeline is concrete and datable:

| Catalyst | Timing | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| VCP Dan Phuong (Hanoi, 25,000 m²) | 3Q2026 | First modern mall in a growing Hanoi district |
| J-Town commercial streets, My Lam (Tuyen Quang) | 4Q2026 | New Vincom Collection format; tourism catchment |
| 764,000 m² secured (deposits) + 980,000 m² under feasibility | 2026–28 | Visibility on the Mega-Mall ramp |
| VMM Apollo City (Quảng Ninh, ~110,000 m²), Green City (Long An), 4 malls at Green Paradise (HCMC) | Post-2026 | The earnings-acceleration leg |
| Shophouse sales — Golden Avenue (~VND1.3tn), Royal Island (~VND4.8tn), Green Paradise (~1,300 units) | 2027+ | Episodic cash-flow boosts; **timing keeps slipping** |

We model the leasing-led case: each m² of Mega-Mall GFA carries high incremental margin because the cost base is largely fixed, so EBITDA should re-accelerate toward its >60% peak as new malls stabilise. The asset-recycling option (the Vincom NCT sale realised a ~VND1,900bn gain on a single asset) and a future REIT vehicle are unpriced sources of NAV.

## 4. Valuation

**Primary — FCFF DCF.** Our target price of **VND34,972** is set on an unlevered three-year explicit DCF (2026–28) plus terminal value, using **WACC 11%** (cost of equity 10% on a 4% risk-free rate, beta 1.0, 5% country risk premium; pre-tax cost of debt 11%) and a **1% terminal growth rate**. This yields an enterprise NPV of ~VND79tn, an equity value of ~VND81tn after net cash, and ~VND35,000/share. The valuation is deliberately conservative: a 1% terminal growth understates the structural Vietnamese consumption story, and the model does not capitalise the REIT/asset-recycling optionality.

**Cross-check — P/B band.** At spot, VRE trades at ~1.1x trailing P/B versus an eight-year mean of ~1.9x (+1SD 2.5x; −2SD 1.1x). Our target sits at only ~1.6x P/B — still below mid-cycle — so we are not relying on a heroic re-rating; mean-reversion plus modest book growth gets us there.

**Cross-check — regional peers.** On normalised earnings VRE trades at ~14–15x P/E, broadly in line with Simon Property (~14x), CapitaLand Integrated (~17x) and Central Pattana (~15x) — but at a P/B discount to Central Pattana (4.4x) and SM Prime (1.7x) despite comparable or better margins. The discount is unjustified given VRE's scale moat and balance-sheet strength.

## 6. Risks and what would change our view

1. **Aeon and the experience gap.** Aeon's free-parking, food-court destination model draws 6–10m visits/site — above Vincom Mega Mall's ~5m. If like-for-like footfall at older Vincom Plazas/Centers turns negative, rent renewals weaken. *Watch: like-for-like (not system-wide) footfall.*
2. **Related-party concentration.** ~VND29.5tn of deposits/intercompany lending and ~90% of the pipeline tied to Vinhomes. A change in ecosystem terms, deposit rates, or handover pace would hit both financial income and the growth engine. *Watch: related-party note disclosures, deposit yields.*
3. **Shophouse-timing slippage.** Golden Avenue and Royal Island have already slipped from 4Q25; further delays push out the 2027 cash-flow leg (our explicit reason for a conservative 2026F).
4. **Investment-phase cash drag.** 2025 operating cash flow was negative (−VND3,262bn) on rising receivables/prepayments; the cycle is funded by asset recycling and debt. *Watch: cash conversion of EBITDA.*

**We would downgrade if** like-for-like footfall turns negative for two consecutive quarters, *or* related-party deposits rise materially further without disclosure of terms, *or* the Mega-Mall pipeline slips beyond 2028.
