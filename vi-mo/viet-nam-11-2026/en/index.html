Vietnam macro 11/2026: rates, inflation, growth and what they mean for sectors

The policy rate is unchanged at 4.5%, but the average lending rate has risen 1.90pp in 11 months, to 9.65%. Inflation is 5.09% while 3Q26 GDP grew 9.95%.

Key points

  • The policy rate has stood still while market rates rose. The refinancing rate has been 4.5% since Jun 2023. Between Oct 2025 and Sep 2026 the average lending rate rose from 7.75% to 9.65% (+1.90pp) and the average deposit rate from 5.50% to 7.00% (+1.50pp).
  • Term funding in the interbank market costs more than a year ago. The 6-month interbank rate is 7.97% (Nov 2026) against 5.29% a year earlier. The overnight rate swung widely in 2026: from 1.19% (Aug 2026) to 12.49% (Jun 2026).
  • Inflation is above the policy rate. CPI rose 5.09% year on year in Oct 2026 (Oct 2025: 3.25%) and has been above the refinancing rate since Aug 2026. Core inflation is 4.04%, down from a peak of 4.67% (May 2026).
  • Growth is strong. GDP grew 9.95% in 3Q26, the fastest since 3Q22; construction +14.2%, manufacturing +12.3%. Retail sales rose 14.74% in Oct 2026 (nominal). FDI disbursed over 10 months was US$24.1bn (+13.1%).
  • Credit is growing faster than money supply. By Aug 2026 credit was up 10.19% on the end of the previous year and M2 up 6.84%; the gap is 3.35pp, against 1.89pp at the same point of 2025.

1 · Rates and liquidity

The refinancing rate is unchanged at 4.5%, but market rates have moved up. Deposit rates for terms over 12 months are 6.2–7.8% (Sep 2026), against 4.9–6.1% a year earlier. In the interbank market the 3-month rate is 7.00% and the 6-month rate 7.97%.

Reading: credit growing faster than money supply (the fifth key point) is consistent with banks paying more to raise deposits. The data here cannot separate causes.

2 · Inflation

CPI rose 5.09% year on year in Oct 2026, 0.59pp above the refinancing rate. Core inflation is 4.04%, 1.05pp below headline.

3 · Growth and demand

GDP grew 9.95% year on year in 3Q26, after 8.81% in 2Q26 and 7.94% in 1Q26. FDI registered over 10 months was US$54.9bn (+74.0%).

4 · What it means for the sectors

For each sector: one observation from the macro data, one reading by the author, and the related report on this site.

Banks

Observed: Between Oct 2025 and Sep 2026 the average lending rate rose 1.90pp and the average deposit rate 1.50pp; the gap between them widened from 2.25pp to 2.65pp.

Reading: These are system-wide average rates, not any bank’s net interest margin: a margin also depends on the deposit mix and on how fast each side reprices. See Banks 1H26: grading the forecasts against reported results.

Securities

Observed: The 3- and 6-month interbank rates are 7.00–7.97% (Nov 2026). Over the 12 months to 3Q26 the securities industry’s cost of funds was 6.4% and its margin-lending yield 11.2%.

Reading: If brokers’ borrowing costs moved to interbank levels with margin yields unchanged, the 4.9pp spread would narrow. This is conditional arithmetic, not a forecast. See Securities sector 3Q26: profit, market share, returns and valuation.

Real estate

Observed: VND medium- and long-term lending rates are 8.4–10.9% (Sep 2026) against 6.5–8.9% a year earlier. Real estate activities grew 5.6% in 3Q26, below the economy’s 9.95%.

Reading: Dearer borrowing reaches both home buyers and developers; the sector report looks at listed developers’ sales and leverage. See Real estate, steel and retail 3Q26: drivers and trends.

Steel and construction

Observed: Construction GDP grew 14.2% in 3Q26, the fastest since 3Q22 (3Q25: 9.2%).

Reading: Construction is accelerating while rates rise; the sector report tracks construction-steel consumption to see which matters more. See Real estate, steel and retail 3Q26: drivers and trends.

Retail

Observed: Retail sales rose 14.74% year on year in Oct 2026 (Oct 2025: 7.23%), in current prices; CPI rose 5.09% in the same month.

Reading: Part of the nominal rise is prices. Retail growth minus CPI (a simple subtraction) leaves 9.65pp, against 3.98pp a year earlier. See Real estate, steel and retail 3Q26: drivers and trends.

Method and sources

  • Source: FiinX macro series, pulled on 10 Nov 2026. Every number on the page is computed from them; the two securities-industry figures come from the securities sector report on this site.
  • Policy and interbank rates: FiinX monthly series; the source does not say whether a value is end-of-period or a monthly average. November 2026 is the figure to 10 Nov. The policy-rate series has no data for March–August 2024.
  • Average lending and deposit rates: available only from Oct 2025 to Sep 2026, so comparisons use Oct 2025 as the base.
  • Credit and M2: growth against the end of the previous year. The source’s credit figure for Sep 2026 (16.69%) does not fit the trend after 10.19% in Aug 2026 and is not used; M2 runs to Aug 2026.
  • GDP: single-quarter growth year on year, constant prices. Retail: total retail sales of goods and services, current prices. FDI: year to date.
  • Not covered: exchange rates, government bond yields, trade and PMI are not in this data set.
  • Reading convention: "Observed" is verifiable from the data; "Reading" is the author’s judgment.
