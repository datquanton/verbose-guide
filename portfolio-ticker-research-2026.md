## 1. KDH — Khang Dien House (44% of book, −27.7%) — thesis under stress

**Results.** 1Q26 core business was weak: revenue VND281bn, **−60% YoY**, with only
6 Gladia units handed over. Reported NPAT +131% YoY (VND281bn to parent) — but driven
by a one-off VND285bn bargain-purchase gain on consolidating An Lap Real Estate
(8.2ha Cat Lai, bought Mar 2026), not operations. Operating cash flow **−VND634bn —
the fifth consecutive negative year**. 2Q26 not yet reported; broker previews diverge
wildly (NPAT VND102bn vs VND348bn) depending on Gladia handover assumptions.
[Sources: vnbusiness.vn](https://vnbusiness.vn/khang-dien-bao-lai-tang-131-nhung-dong-tien-kinh-doanh-am-634-ty-dong.html), [elibook](https://elibook.vn/2026/05/09/kdh-chi-ban-giao-6-can-gladia-trong-q1-nhung-loi-nhuan-van-tang-manh-131-yoy-nho-mua-re-8-2-ha-dat-o-cat-lai.html)

**Why it fell 52% from the Sep-2025 peak (the part v1 couldn't see):**
1. **Bond violations**: Government Inspectorate Conclusion 46/KL-TTCP (Feb 2026) found
   dishonest bond-offering documents and misuse of proceeds (>VND100bn used to repay
   debt); escaped an SSC fine only because the statute of limitations expired
   ([fili.vn](https://fili.vn/2026/05/bi-thanh-tra-chinh-phu-neu-loat-vi-pham-ve-trai-phieu-khang-dien-thoat-an-phat-cua-ubcknn-do-het-thoi-hieu-3118-1442025.htm)).
2. **VNDiamond index removal**: FOL fell below threshold → weight halved at the May
   rebalance (4m+ shares of ETF selling), formal removal likely at the July review.
3. **Institutional distribution**: VinaCapital below 7%, Dragon Capital down to ~6%.
4. **Balance-sheet optics**: liabilities +42% YTD to VND18.3tn (borrowings ~VND15.3tn,
   implied net debt ~VND11.5tn), inventory at a record **VND29.1tn = 73% of assets**.

**Street (with staleness flags).** KBSV BUY TP 36,200 (Mar 18); Vietcap BUY TP 33,000
(mid-May); **SSI TP 27,300 — the lowest and freshest print**. The "consensus ~38,850,
9 buys / 0 sells" from v1 predates the July collapse — treat it as stale. Valuation
now ~0.85–1.0x book (my estimate) — distressed-developer territory for a company with
600ha+ of clean HCMC land.

**Offsets / bull case still alive:** Gladia Heights launched with Keppel (June 12,
sales from Q3; 2BR from ~VND6.5bn); Clarita 51% divestment brings VND1,743bn cash in
Q3; gross margins on the key projects estimated 52–53%; management targets NPAT
VND1.5tn (stretch 2.0–2.5tn); the founder's son registered to buy 20m shares
(Jul 23–Aug 21) — meaningful insider signal at ~VND360–400bn.

**Buy-side view (revised):** this is no longer just "market prices execution risk" —
there are real governance (bonds), flow (index/ETF), and cash-flow issues. The
position is 44% of the book. The disciplined move set: (a) do nothing before the 2Q
report and Gladia Heights launch data, (b) use any insider-buying / Q3-catalyst
strength to cut the weight toward a size where a further −30% doesn't define the
portfolio, (c) re-underwrite only on evidence of actual presales absorption, not
handover accounting. Averaging down here is adding to a governance-flagged,
cash-burning position — the research does not support it.
