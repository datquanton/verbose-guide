## 7. Portfolio-level conclusions (the buy-side memo)

1. **The book is one macro trade expressed six ways.** Every position is long Vietnam
   domestic liquidity + the FTSE upgrade cycle. If SBV tightens harder or the upgrade
   disappoints, nothing in the book hedges anything else. Recognize it and size for it.
2. **KDH sizing is the #1 decision.** Fundamentals and the entire street support the
   name; nothing supports 44% in a pre-catalyst developer. The 2H26 Gladia launches
   are the exit/right-size window the research points to.
3. **Ecosystem double-counting.** TCB+TCX (33%) and VPB+VPX (23%) each own the same
   earnings stream twice — the banks consolidate their brokers. Choose the instrument:
   banks for carry + quality, brokers for the September FTSE event — holding both
   pairs at full weight is leverage, not diversification.
4. **The calendar is doing the underwriting.** Sept 21, 2026 (FTSE effective date) is
   the single most important date in the book: it is the bull catalyst for TCX, VPX,
   and indirectly TCB/VPB. Positions should have a pre-September plan and a
   post-September plan.
5. **What would change the picture** (watch quarterly): Gladia presales (KDH), FE
   Credit NPL formation (VPB), margin-debt growth vs VN-Index (TCX/VPX cycle heat),
   deposit-rate trajectory (all banks), SBV quota revisions mid-year.
6. **Averaging down:** the research would only defend it on TCB and VPB (widest
   consensus upside, strongest balance-sheet support). On KDH it increases an already
   outsized bet; on TCX/VPX it doubles an event trade; on HPG it's a new decision,
   not an average.
