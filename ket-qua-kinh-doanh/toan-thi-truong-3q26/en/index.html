Listed-market earnings 3Q26: 19 sectors, drivers and concentration

After-tax profit of 1,133 listed companies rose 46.0% year on year but was flat on the previous quarter; VIC and VHM alone produced 53% of the increase. Share prices fell anyway: the VN-Index P/E went from 15.9x at end-2025 to 10.8x.

Key points

  • Growth is high and concentrated. Market profit in 3Q26 was 245.3tn; on the same set of companies it was up 77.1tn on a year earlier. Five companies (VIC, VHM, BSR, HPG, MBB) produced 72% of the increase. Excluding VIC and VHM, profit rose 22.4%.
  • Quarter-on-quarter momentum has stalled. Against 2Q26 market profit was +0.1%; excluding VIC and VHM, −6.5%. Banks −4.3%, financial services −26.3%, utilities −14.0%.
  • Three sources of growth: property handovers, commodity margins, bank lending. Real estate 12.4tn → 54.6tn; oil & gas plus basic resources 8.1tn → 27.0tn; banks +21.1%.
  • Eight sectors earned less. The largest falls in value were financial services (−45.5%, against the brokers’ high 3Q25 base), construction & materials (−29.5%) and industrial goods & services (−14.2%).
  • Earnings up, multiples down. The VN-Index is −2.8% year to date while nine-month profit rose 43.9%; the P/E fell from 15.9x to 10.8x. The average lending rate rose 1.90pp over the same period (see the macro page).

3 · Concentration: who produced the increase

Observed: Of the market’s 77.1tn increase, 40.7tn came from VIC and VHM (53%). 596 of 1,111 companies (54%) earned more than a year earlier.

Reading: The market sum double-counts: VHM is a subsidiary of VIC, so VHM’s profit is already inside VIC’s consolidated figure. The same holds for other listed subsidiaries (for example DMX within MWG). The “excluding VIC and VHM” figure is therefore the closer measure of the rest of the market, and the total is best used only for comparison across periods.

4 · What drove each sector

For each sector: one observation from the data (the companies that moved sector profit most) and one reading by the author.

Banks

Observed: Net profit 69.7tn → 84.4tn (+21.1% year on year, −4.3% on the quarter). Largest increases: MBB (5,571 → 8,040); CTG (8,449 → 10,835); BID (5,953 → 8,331). Largest decreases: STB (2,901 → 1,347); EIB (445 → 144). Figures in brackets: VND bn.

Reading: Core income kept growing but provisions grew faster, so profit fell on the previous quarter. Margins, bad debt and valuation are in Banks 3Q26.

Real Estate

Observed: Net profit 12.4tn → 54.6tn (+340.4% year on year, +22.1% on the quarter). Largest increases: VIC (640 → 22,354); VHM (4,184 → 23,146); NVL (−878 → 1,119). Largest decreases: KSF (1,358 → 34); HDC (538 → 63). Figures in brackets: VND bn.

Reading: This is profit recognised on 2025 sales, concentrated in two companies. Real estate, steel and retail 3Q26 shows the indicators of new sales have already peaked.

Oil & Gas

Observed: Net profit 2.6tn → 11.6tn (+346.0% year on year, +2.8% on the quarter). Largest increases: BSR (908 → 8,968); PLX (611 → 1,241); OIL (125 → 716). Largest decreases: PVD (278 → 71); TOS (308 → 253). Figures in brackets: VND bn.

Reading: Sector profit is almost one refiner’s, whose gross margin rose from 2.9% to 16.2% (see the VN30 report). The sector index has fallen 14.3% since 30 Sep despite a P/E of 6.0x: the market is treating this margin as a cyclical peak.

Basic Resources

Observed: Net profit 5.5tn → 15.4tn (+178.3% year on year, +33.4% on the quarter). Largest increases: HPG (3,988 → 8,217); MSR (5 → 2,148); KSV (595 → 2,409). Largest decreases: HGM (116 → 21); HLC (73 → 23). Figures in brackets: VND bn.

Reading: Steel (HPG) follows volume and new capacity; mining (MSR, KSV) follows metal prices. Steel is covered in Real estate, steel and retail 3Q26.

Utilities

Observed: Net profit 8.7tn → 13.8tn (+58.1% year on year, −14.0% on the quarter). Largest increases: GAS (2,549 → 4,576); PGV (165 → 1,221); POW (809 → 1,860). Largest decreases: DTK (−3 → −160); HDG (285 → 163). Figures in brackets: VND bn.

Reading: Up year on year but down on the quarter; gas and power are seasonal and depend on input prices, so one quarter does not establish a trend.

Food & Beverage

Observed: Net profit 11.5tn → 14.6tn (+26.6% year on year, −1.8% on the quarter). Largest increases: MSN (1,209 → 3,671); VNM (2,527 → 2,964); IDP (−134 → 224). Largest decreases: VHC (433 → 213); DBC (343 → 200). Figures in brackets: VND bn.

Reading: Growth came from a few large companies; sector profit was flat on the quarter. The sector P/E fell from 20.6x to 12.7x during the year.

Retail

Observed: Net profit 3.9tn → 7.0tn (+80.0% year on year, −0.6% on the quarter). Largest increases: DMX (1,380 → 2,825); MWG (1,771 → 3,094); FRT (219 → 440). Largest decreases: PET (105 → 69); SBV (11 → −10). Figures in brackets: VND bn.

Reading: Modern retail keeps growing revenue and widening margins. The sector total double-counts DMX with MWG. See Real estate, steel and retail 3Q26.

Financial Services

Observed: Net profit 14.3tn → 7.8tn (−45.5% year on year, −26.3% on the quarter). Largest increases: ABW (46 → 316); TCX (1,620 → 1,748); F88 (224 → 319). Largest decreases: VIX (2,449 → 174); VPX (1,892 → 670). Figures in brackets: VND bn.

Reading: 3Q25 was an unusually strong quarter for brokers’ investment income; the fall reflects the base more than margin lending. See Securities sector 3Q26 (Vietnamese).

Technology

Observed: Net profit 2.7tn → 2.9tn (+10.2% year on year, +4.3% on the quarter). Largest increases: FPT (2,435 → 2,723); SAM (12 → 41); POT (1 → 13). Largest decreases: SMT (45 → 0); SBD (20 → 9). Figures in brackets: VND bn.

Reading: Sector revenue fell because FPT stopped consolidating its telecom arm from the start of 2026, not because demand weakened; profit still rose. The sector index is down 40.7% year to date. See VN30 3Q26.

Construction & Materials

Observed: Net profit 7.9tn → 5.7tn (−29.5% year on year, −0.5% on the quarter). Largest increases: VGC (92 → 464); SJG (288 → 609); DFF (−368 → −156). Largest decreases: VCG (3,282 → 306); CTX (216 → 11). Figures in brackets: VND bn.

Reading: The fall is mostly one large profit in 3Q25 that did not repeat; sector revenue still rose 14.1%.

Industrial Goods & Services

Observed: Net profit 12.0tn → 10.4tn (−14.2% year on year, −24.2% on the quarter). Largest increases: PHP (261 → 406); GMD (315 → 444); PVT (263 → 375). Largest decreases: GEE (1,744 → 774); ACV (2,767 → 2,206). Figures in brackets: VND bn.

Reading: Sector revenue rose 23.3% but profit fell, mostly at two large companies (GEE, ACV). The data here does not show why; each company’s notes need reading.

5 · Prices and valuation

Observed: P/E fell in 16 of 19 sectors against end-2025; 11 of 19 sector indices are down year to date. VN-Index: P/E 15.9x → 10.8x, P/B 2.22 → 1.85. VN30 −9.4% year to date.

Reading: When earnings rise and prices fall, the market is either demanding a higher return (rates have risen) or doubting that current earnings will hold (property handovers and commodity margins are both cyclical). The data here cannot separate the two; both are consistent with quarterly profit having flattened.

Method and sources

  • Source: FiinQuantX, pulled 13 Nov 2026. Single-quarter income statements (not cumulative), consolidated as chosen by the provider; 3Q26 statements are mostly unaudited.
  • Coverage: 1,133 of 1,541 listed tickers had 3Q26 statements on the pull date, 99.1% of market cap. 24 fund certificates classified under financial services file no income statement.
  • Profit: net profit attributable to parent shareholders. Growth: computed on the same set of companies with data in both periods; nine-month figures use companies with all six quarters. No % is given when the base is zero or negative.
  • Double counting: sector totals add every listed company, including listed subsidiaries of listed parents (VHM in VIC, DMX in MWG). The page does not adjust for this; it only adds the “excluding VIC and VHM” figure.
  • Sectors: ICB level 2. P/E, P/B, sector price indices: the data provider’s; their method (universe, earnings basis) is not stated in the source. Comparison dates: 31 Dec 2025, 30 Jun 2026, 30 Sep 2026 and 12 Nov 2026.
  • Data notes: retail 4Q25 lacks DMX; some companies’ fiscal quarters do not match the calendar year (the “3Q26” label is the provider’s); technology revenue growth is affected by FPT’s change in consolidation scope.
  • Reading convention: “Observed” is verifiable from the data; “Reading” is the author’s judgment.
