# Annual Report Reading Program

The highest-return research activity: primary documents, read slowly, notes filed.
Sell-side digests ARs for you and loses exactly the parts that matter (auditor notes,
related parties, segment economics, commitments). This program covers **holdings →
listed competitors → big private competitors**, in that order.

Notes go to `research/annual-reports/notes/<TICKER>-AR<year>.md` using
`research/templates/ar-notes.md`. One AR ≈ 2–4 hours done properly. Cadence: one per
week sustains the whole program; holdings first pass ≈ 6 weeks.

## How to read an AR (order matters)

1. **Auditor's opinion + basis** — qualified? emphasis of matter? change of auditor? (NKG's auditor was suspended by the SSC over its FY25 signature — this is exactly the kind of thing found here first)
2. **Financial-statement NOTES, before the statements**: related-party transactions, commitments & contingencies, pledged assets, debt maturity schedule, segment note
3. **Cash-flow statement** — 5 years side by side; profits are an opinion, cash is a fact
4. **Balance sheet** — what grew faster than revenue? (receivables, inventory, "other assets" = the hiding places)
5. **Income statement** — last among the numbers; strip one-offs as you go
6. **Chairman/CEO letter + strategy section** — read LAST, as a narrative-vs-numbers consistency check. Note what they *stop* talking about vs last year's letter
7. Governance section: board changes, ESOP grants, insider/major-shareholder moves, audit-committee activity

Per company below: the specific items to hunt for.

## Tier 2 — Listed competitors (comparative pass: same metrics, side by side)

*Comparative read: CASA vs TCB, credit-cost through the cycle, real-estate + bond exposure, fee mix.*

**Brokers (vs TCX/VPX):** SSI ([ssi.com.vn IR](https://www.ssi.com.vn/en/investor-relation)) · VCI · HCM · VND · MBS.
*Comparative read: margin-book/equity headroom, prop-book size, fee-war exposure, funding cost.*

**Developers (vs KDH):** NLG · VHM ([vinhomes.vn AR page](https://vinhomes.vn/en/bao-cao-thuong-nien)) · DXG · PDR · NVL (read NVL as the cautionary control case: what distress looks like in the notes 2 years early).
*Comparative read: inventory turns, advances/inventory ratio, capitalized interest policy, presales disclosure quality.*

**Steel (vs HPG):** HSG ([annual reports](https://hoasengroup.vn/en/shareholders/annual-reports/28/), FY ends September) · NKG ([Vietstock docs](https://finance.vietstock.vn/nkg/tai-tai-lieu.htm) — note the FY25 auditor-suspension issue) · GDA.
*Comparative read: spread capture vs HPG's integration, export mix & AD-duty exposure, inventory-cycle management.*

Keppel's own results releases disclose Gladia JV progress — an independent check on KDH's telling.

## Filing discipline

- One notes file per AR (template: `research/templates/ar-notes.md`); link it from the ticker dossier
- Every AR read updates the dossier's governance + earnings-quality sections same day
- Flag anything that contradicts the current thesis in `monitoring-log.md` immediately
