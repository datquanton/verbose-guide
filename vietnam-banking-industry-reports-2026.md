## 1. Securities firms — sector reports

### MBS Research (MB Securities) — "Vietnam Dynamics 2026 | Banking Sector: Strategic Efforts Toward Sustaining Double-Digit Growth"
- **Link (PDF):** https://www.mbs.com.vn/files/uploads/2025/12/Navi_2026_Banking_Strategic-Efforts-Toward-Sustaining-Double-Digit-Growth.pdf
- **Published:** December 2025 (2026 annual strategy)
- **Key forecasts:**
  - After-tax profit of covered banks to grow ~**20.5%** in 2026.
  - Sector-wide NIM broadly flat or slightly lower in 2026; partial support from medium/long-term loans repricing onto floating rates after preferential periods end.
  - Credit expansion led by private commercial banks (+16.7% YTD by end-3Q25 vs. +12.0% for state-owned banks).
  - Average 12-month deposit rate held ~4.8% through 9M25, rising sharply from October 2025.
  - MBBank forecast to lead the industry in ROE at **20.2%**, followed by VIB (17.5%), ACB (17.2%), VCB (16.9%), TPBank (16.9%), VPBank (16.9%).
- **Top picks:** CTG, ACB, BID — public-investment lending momentum, low cost of funds, capital increase plans.

## 2. Rating agencies & credit research

### FiinRatings — "Vietnam Banking Sector: FY2025 Recap and 2026 Outlook" (Hanoi, 13 Apr 2026)
- **Links:**
  - English PDF: https://fiinratings.vn/Data/2026/Report/FiinRatings_Vietnam-Banking-2026-Outlook.pdf
  - Vietnamese PDF: https://fiinratings.vn/Data/2026/Report/FiinRatings_Tam-diem-nganh-Ngan-hang-2026.pdf
  - Summary: https://fiinratings.vn/en/news-fg/banking-sector-2026-spotlight-capital-strength-and-liquidity-will-shape-vietnam-banking-outlook-id2772252.html
- **Key points:**
  - Sector NIM to stay **below 3%** in 2026 (2.9% in 2025, down from 3.5% in 2024) as late-2025 liquidity pressures keep pushing up funding costs.
  - 2025 credit growth of 19% far exceeded deposit growth of 11.4% — structural liquidity pressure; heavy reliance on wholesale funding and short-term (<1Y) deposits; banks likely to raise deposit rates for longer tenors.
  - Bad-debt pressure could rise in 2026: 2025 NPL stability was driven by write-offs/recoveries, and provisioning buffers are at their lowest since 2021.
  - Winners: banks with strong capital, high CASA, and international fundraising capability.

### VIS Rating (Moody's-backed) — "Banking Sector — 3M2026 Update"
- **Link:** https://visrating.com/research/banking-sector-3m2026-update.351.html
- **Key points:**
  - Banks entered 2026 with a tighter, more volatile operating environment; elevated rates weaken asset quality and profitability.
  - 1Q26: sector ROAA fell 10bps QoQ to **1.4%**; NIM narrowed ~11bps on funding costs — worst at banks with weak deposit franchises.
  - Mid-sized banks saw the sharpest credit deterioration (rising retail delinquencies); large/state-owned banks broadly stable on diversified books and fee income.
  - Downside risks from persistently high rates and rising household leverage, especially for retail-heavy banks with thin buffers.

### Consensus themes for 2026
1. **Credit quotas are the new profit driver** — SBV target ~15% (most banks allocated 11–13%; restructuring participants get more; VPBank guiding 35%). NIM expansion no longer carries earnings.
2. **Liquidity is the binding constraint** — credit growth has outrun deposits for years (LDR ~111–112%); funding costs rising, deposit-rate competition back (top rates ~9%).
3. **NIM compression** — sector NIM ~2.9% in 2025 (from 3.5% in 2024); most houses see sub-3% or flat-at-best in 2026.
4. **Divergence** — large/state-owned banks (diversified books, CASA, fee income) resilient; mid-sized retail-heavy banks face rising delinquencies and falling ROAA.
5. **Asset-quality watch** — provisioning buffers at post-2021 lows; 2025 NPL stability owed to write-offs, so bad-debt pressure may resurface in 2026.
6. **Still double-digit profit growth** — consensus sector profit growth clusters at **+16% to +20%** for 2026.
