Key points

  • Most of the gap was there on the day Mirae Asset published. On the 25 recent targets not flagged for a cut, the median gap was +30.0% at the report date and is +37.5% now: falling prices added only about 8 points. It is Mirae Asset's conviction, not cushion the market has confirmed.
  • Four drivers cut across industries. Interest rates (bank funding costs; financial expenses at GEG, HPG, ANV and TNG), oil (around US$90-100 a barrel), El Niño (hydropower against thermal) and US tariffs (12.5% from 24 Jul 2026, now settled).
  • The thickest cushion is net cash. 14 of the 32 non-bank companies hold more cash than debt in the latest figures Mirae Asset prints: VNM, SAB, QNS, MSH, VHC, PLX, PVS, NT2, DCM, FPT, CTR, GMD, CTD, NLG.
  • The thinnest cushion is leverage plus forecasts about to be cut: GEG (net debt to equity 98%, interest cover 1.9x), TNG and STK in textiles, and the banks with loan-loss coverage below 50% (HDB, VIB, EIB, STB).
  • The next five weeks are the test. Property and industrial-land handovers, PVS's project acceptance, banks' 4Q26 funding costs and textile orders all report before the 2026 books close.
How to read: drivers, milestones, cushion

Underlying drivers are the few variables that set a company's earnings (volume, price, margin, funding cost). Milestones are dated, checkable events that show whether the thesis is still on track. Cushion is what limits the fall if the drivers disappoint: valuation, balance sheet, earnings already secured and dividends. The gap to target price is only one part of the cushion, and it means something only while the target is fresh.

Consumer staples and retail: milestones, drivers and cushion

6 · How this was built

The Sector & Firm section of Mirae Asset Vietnam's site holds 815 English reports. We took every 2026 report, plus the latest full 2025 report for tickers with little coverage this year, and read the 115 reports used for the 44 stocks and the sector reports that go with them.

Every figure on these pages was checked word for word against the source report text before publication. The newest report on a ticker governs; older ones show how the target moved. Where a target changed because the share count rose (bonus shares, stock dividends, new issues), the note under the ticker says so.

Note: the forward P/E and P/B Mirae Asset prints in its tables are usually computed at the target price, not the market price. These pages therefore use trailing P/E and P/B at market price from FiinQuantX.
