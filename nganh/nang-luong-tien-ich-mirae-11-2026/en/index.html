Oil, gas, fertiliser, power and water: milestones, drivers and cushion

El Niño shifts output from hydropower to gas- and coal-fired plants until at least 1Q27, helping NT2 and hurting VSH, HDG and GEG. In fuel, the shake-out of distributors lifts PLX and OIL volumes. Net cash at PLX, PVS, NT2 and DCM is the thickest cushion in the group.

Key points

  • Power: hydrology picks the winners. System output rose about 10% over eight months and the full market price averaged +37%. With hydropower down, EVN dispatches thermal plants: Mirae Asset raised NT2's 2026-27 profit forecasts by 34-35%, while VSH's 3Q26 volume fell 44%.
  • Fuel: regulation is the driver. Average petroleum prices rose 89% over nine months; tighter oversight removed small distributors and moved volume to PLX and OIL. The market-pricing decree (expected October 2026) and the end of tax relief after 30 Sep 2026 are not confirmed in the reports.
  • Upstream: earnings follow acceptance schedules, not oil prices. PVS's nine-month core earnings are only 45% of its full-year forecast, so 2026 depends on year-end acceptance; Block B is due to deliver first gas in 2H27.
  • The cushion splits sharply. PLX (net debt to equity −0.3x), PVS (−0.7x), NT2 (−62%) and DCM hold net cash. GEG is thinnest: net debt to equity 98% and interest cover 1.9x; HDG's leverage rises to 84% by 2028 to build wind farms.
  • Four targets need a discount. GEG, HDG, VSH and DCM were restated in flashes that say forecasts will be cut or are under review; BWE's target dates from August 2026.

How to read this. Underlying drivers are the few variables that set a company's earnings (volume, price, margin, funding cost). Milestones are dated, checkable events that show whether the thesis is still on track. Cushion is what limits the fall if the drivers disappoint: valuation, balance sheet, earnings already secured and dividends. The gap to target price is only one part of the cushion, and it means something only while the target is fresh.

1 · Industry: what drives earnings now

Oil, gas and fertiliser

No oil and gas sector report is among those read here; the only sector call is Neutral on agrichemicals (16 Jan 2026). By stock: Buy on PLX and PVS, Trading Buy on GAS and OIL, Hold on DCM. Fuel retail reform and year-end upstream handovers support the first group; falling urea prices weigh on DCM.

  • Fuel prices: average petroleum prices rose 89% YoY in 9M26. 9M26 revenue grew 64% YoY at PLX and 115% at OIL, with large inventory provision swings (PLX: VND6,322bn charge in 1Q26, VND6,074bn reversal in 2Q26).
  • Fuel retail regulation: tighter oversight removed about 19 distributor licences by end-August 2026 (-8% YTD). Volume moved to the large wholesalers: 9M26 volume rose 16% YoY at PLX (46% market share) and 25% YoY at OIL.
  • Gas supply mix: LNG is replacing declining domestic gas. GAS sold 5.6bn m³ in 9M26 (+19% YoY) and gas-fired generation reached 20.5bn kWh (+38% YoY), but LNG earns a 6.8% gross margin against 24.5% for dry gas (1H26).
  • Upstream project execution: PVS earnings follow project handover schedules more than spot Brent. Block B revenue booked to end-2025 was VND14,401bn (about 53% of the EPC package); M&C revenue was VND17,332bn in 9M26 (+13% YoY).
  • Urea price and policy: Middle East urea fell 29% QoQ (-9% YoY) in 3Q26. China reimposed export curbs in October 2025; fertiliser carries 5% VAT with input deduction since 1 July 2025; domestic gas prices are expected to cap input costs.

Power and water utilities

Mirae keeps a Neutral stance on the electricity sector (30 Sep 2026): hydropower Negative as El Niño is expected to persist through 1Q27, coal-fired thermal Positive on high dispatch, gas-fired thermal and renewables Neutral. Cash dividends are the defensive feature while interest rate pressure rises.

  • Power demand: system generation reached about 235bn kWh in 8M26 (+10% YoY), in line with MOIT's 2026 plan of 350bn kWh in the base case (+9% YoY) and 360bn kWh in the system operation scenario (+12% YoY).
  • Hydrology (ENSO): hydropower output fell to 58bn kWh in 8M26 (-7% YoY, 25% of the mix) while coal-fired rose to 119bn kWh (+13%) and gas-fired to 18bn kWh (+37%); June-August 2026 river flows ran 10%-75% below the multi-year average.
  • Market prices: the Full Market Price averaged VND1,427/kWh in 8M26 (+37% YoY). 2026 generation price ceilings: coal-fired VND2,219/kWh (+30% YoY), natural gas VND3,410.6/kWh (+11%), hydropower VND1,110/kWh (unchanged).
  • Gas supply and contracted output (Qc): domestic gas prices fell 7%-8% by July 2026 from March highs and NT2 paid US$9.99/MMBTU in 9M26 (+6% YoY); Mirae holds NT2's Qc ratio at 90%, while LNG plants NT3&4 have a 65% minimum Qc under Decree 100/2025.
  • Renewable tariffs: 2026 solar and wind price brackets are under MOIT review; FIT eligibility remains a risk (HDG booked a VND193bn provision in 1Q26 for the gap between FIT1 and the transitional price at Infra 1). Grid-connected DPPA was first commercialised in June 2026.

2 · Cushion: price against target, and what sits behind it

PVS and PLX have the largest gaps (+71% and +61%) and both hold net cash, but each carries a condition: PVS needs year-end acceptance on time, and PLX needs no repeat of the large 1Q26 inventory provision (net margin is only 0.9%). NT2 has a +30% gap, cash equal to 48% of total assets and a dividend of about VND1,500 a share, but its earnings depend on El Niño. For GEG, HDG, VSH and DCM, do not count the gap to target as cushion until Mirae Asset updates its forecasts.

GASPetroVietnam Gas JSC (PV GAS)Trading Buy

Target price 92,200 (4 Nov 2026) · price 69,300 · gap to target +33% · 9M26 profit 75% · Not stated · P/E 11.3x · P/B 2.28x

Trading Buy, 22% expected return: 9M26 net profit rose 33% YoY to VND13,381bn (75% of FY26F) as LNG and Nhon Trach 3&4 revived gas-fired power demand. The 3Q26 slowdown is seen as seasonal; LNG adds more volume than profit.

* Rating and target are as printed in the header of the 4 Nov 2026 earnings update, which has no valuation section. The rating history table in the same note lists only one earlier entry (7/11/2024, Trading Buy, VND88,800), so the date the VND92,200 target was set cannot be confirmed from these reports.

Underlying drivers

  • Gas volume: 2.0bn m³ in 3Q26 (+24% YoY, -6% QoQ) and 5.6bn m³ in 9M26 (+19% YoY), 74% of the FY26F assumption of 7.5bn m³. LNG is the main source of growth, offsetting the natural decline in domestic gas.
  • Gas-fired power demand: generation recovered to 20.5bn kWh in 9M26 (+38% YoY), above the full-year 2025 level, with Nhon Trach 3&4 providing nearly 67% of the increase. In 3Q26 it fell 19% QoQ as hydropower output rose 62% QoQ.
  • Gas margin and LNG mix: gas segment gross margin narrowed 3.7p YoY to 18.3% in 9M26. LNG earned 6.8% in 1H26 against 24.5% for dry gas, so a rising LNG share pressures margin. FY26F group gross margin: 15.4%.
  • LPG: 3Q26 revenue fell 26% YoY to VND16,041bn, yet gross profit doubled YoY as margin recovered to 9.5% from 3.4% in 3Q25. 9M26 LPG revenue of VND46,067bn (-8% YoY) is 61% of FY26F.

Milestones

  • 4Q26 Forecast checkpoint: 9M26 revenue of VND120,977bn and net profit of VND13,381bn are 71% and 75% of FY26F (VND169,832bn and VND17,925bn); 4Q26 has to deliver the remainder.
  • 4Q26 Test of the seasonal reading: gas volume fell 6% QoQ in 3Q26 as EVN prioritised hydropower in the rainy season; FY26F assumes 7.5bn m³ against 5.6bn m³ sold in 9M26.
  • 2026-27 Threshold to watch: utilisation of the 1mtpa Thi Vai LNG terminal, which was running at 40-50% of capacity at the time of the 3Q26 update.
  • 2027 The gas supply plan agreed between PV GAS and EVNGENCO3 for the Phu My power complex starts from 2027, combining domestic gas with regasified LNG.

Cushion

  • Earnings tracking: 9M26 net profit equals 75% of FY26F, and gas segment revenue (VND69,619bn, +86% YoY) equals 81% of forecast.
  • Second profit source: 3Q26 LPG gross profit doubled YoY at a 9.5% margin, while the gas segment supplied 67% of consolidated gross profit.
  • Thin points: LNG's 6.8% gross margin (1H26) dilutes growth, and the 3Q26 update gives no balance-sheet data (cash, debt), so leverage cannot be assessed from these reports.
  • Share price at the update: VND75,600 against a 52-week range of VND60,100 to VND131,500, with a 22% expected return to the VND92,200 target.

What breaks the thesis: A rising LNG share compresses gross margin faster than volume grows (gas segment margin already down 3.7p YoY to 18.3% in 9M26), or gas-fired dispatch stays weak beyond the rainy season as hydropower is prioritised and domestic gas supply keeps declining.

PLXVietnam National Petroleum Group (Petrolimex)Buy

Target price 49,100 (3 Nov 2026) · price 30,500 · gap to target +61% · 9M26 profit 79% · Net cash · P/E 10.2x · P/B 1.42x

Buy: market-based pricing reform and the shake-out of distributors reinforce PLX's 46% market share. 9M26 volume rose 16% YoY, 3Q26 was the first 2026 quarter without a significant inventory provision, and 9M26 net profit reached 79% of FY26F.

Underlying drivers

  • Sales volume: record 3.4mn m³/tons in 3Q26 (+21% YoY) and 9.8mn in 9M26 (+16% YoY); 10M26 reached 10.8mn (+15% YoY), 85% of FY26F. Diesel grew 25% YoY in 9M26 against estimated market growth of 6%.
  • Gross margin and inventory provisions: 3Q26 gross margin 5.3% (-0.1ppt YoY), 5.2% excluding a VND66bn net provision reversal (+4.1ppt QoQ). 1Q26 carried a record VND6,322bn provision, 2Q26 a VND6,074bn reversal. FY26F gross margin: 4.0%.
  • Selling prices: average base selling prices rose an estimated 73% YoY in 3Q26, lifting revenue to VND138,482bn (+66% YoY). Mirae forecasts FY26F revenue of VND547,594bn, then VND506,379bn in FY27F (-7.5%).
  • Supply mix and costs: imports averaged 52% of supply in 3Q26 (58% peak in July); in October domestic procurement rose to 0.74mn m³/tons (+31% YoY). Selling expenses rose 43% YoY to VND5,271bn in 3Q26.

Milestones

  • 4Q26 To meet FY26F net profit of VND4,140bn, 4Q26 needs only VND862bn (+53% YoY, -31% QoQ), after VND3,278bn in 9M26 (+55% YoY).
  • Dec 2026 Full-year volume: 10M26 volume of 10.8mn m³/tons is 85% of the FY26F assumption of 12.8mn; domestic supply availability in 4Q26 decides whether lower import reliance continues.
  • after 30 Sep 2026 (outcome not in reports) Tax relief on petroleum (import tariffs, environmental protection tax, VAT) was scheduled to expire at end-September 2026. Mirae expects a phased roll-back; full reinstatement could raise domestic prices by about 18-36%.
  • Oct 2026 (expected; not confirmed) New petroleum decree expected by October 2026 at the latest, replacing Decrees 83/2014, 95/2021 and 80/2023: companies set retail prices on actual costs, tighter rules for key wholesalers, one "distributor" category. The 3 Nov 2026 note does not confirm issuance.

Cushion

  • Earnings buffer: 9M26 net profit is 79% of FY26F, so the implied 4Q26 requirement of VND862bn leaves room for inventory provisions or pricing-policy changes.
  • Balance sheet: net cash. Cash and short-term investments were VND28,533bn at end-2025 against VND18,085bn of short-term and VND609bn of long-term financial liabilities; net debt-to-equity is -0.3x (2025 and FY26F).
  • Scale: a 46% market share and directly owned infrastructure let PLX fill supply gaps and keep distribution discounts low relative to peers.
  • Thin points: net margin is 0.9% (9M26), so provisions swing results (1Q26: VND6,322bn). The share price of VND33,000 on 29 Oct 2026 was near the 52-week low of VND31,900; forecast dividend is VND1,200 per share.

What breaks the thesis: Inventory provisioning returns after a sharp or exceptional fuel price adjustment (as in 1H26), or pricing policy turns against retailers before the market-based decree takes effect. Mirae already forecasts lower FY27F net profit (VND3,945bn against VND4,140bn in FY26F).

OILPetroVietnam Oil Corporation (PVOIL)Trading Buy

Target price 14,300 (30 Oct 2026) · price 11,900 · gap to target +20% · 9M26 profit 64% · Not stated · P/E 10.3x · P/B 1.09x

Trading Buy: 3Q26 net profit of VND716bn (+472% YoY) nearly doubled 1H26, on 25% volume growth as non-compliant distributors exit, gross margin recovering to 2.7%, steadier domestic pricing and reliable refinery supply. 9M26 net profit is 64% of FY26F.

* Rating and target are as printed in the header of the 30 Oct 2026 earnings update, which has no valuation section. The only full report read here (26 May 2025, 17 months earlier) had a VND11,900 target, shown as VND11,650 after adjustment in the later history table; the date of the move to VND14,300 is not shown.

Underlying drivers

  • Volume and network: estimated 1.9mn m³/ton in 3Q26 (+25% YoY) and 5.3mn in 9M26 (+25% YoY), 76% of the FY26F assumption of 7.0mn. The COCO network reached 1,082 stations (+14% YTD).
  • Gross margin: 2.7% in 3Q26, up from the 2Q26 trough of 1.5% but below 3.1% in 3Q25. Excluding a VND152bn net inventory provision reversal it was 2.5%, in line with the historical average. FY26F: 2.8%.
  • Revenue mix: international trading brought VND46,159bn in 3Q26 (+369% YoY), 51% of revenue for the first time. It is thin-margin, so it raises turnover (9M26: VND226,976bn, +115% YoY) more than profit.
  • Pricing policy: a voluntary retail discount of VND350/liter is in place to contain pump prices. Mirae estimates each full month costs about VND90bn of net profit, around 5% of its FY26 forecast.

Milestones

  • 4Q26 To meet FY26F net profit of VND1,731bn, 4Q26 needs VND619bn, about 14% below the VND716bn reported for 3Q26; 9M26 delivered VND1,100bn (+231% YoY).
  • 4Q26 Whether the temporary VND350/liter voluntary retail discount is extended: each full month would cut net profit by an estimated VND90bn.
  • 1H26 plan; 2031 target Jet fuel: licensed in January 2025; management planned to start in 1H26 with 0.4mn tons and reach 2mn tons by 2031 (May 2025 report). The October 2026 update gives no status.
  • Oct 2026 (expected; not confirmed) New petroleum decree moving to market-based pricing (per Mirae's PLX report of 29 Sep 2026). The May 2025 OIL report expected a CPI-linked adjustment of regulated operating costs to support gross margin.

Cushion

  • Earnings quality: excluding the 3Q26 provision reversal, gross margin was still 2.5%; cumulative inventory write-down provisions were only VND32bn at end-3Q26 (VND184bn at end-2Q26).
  • Forecast buffer: the 4Q26 net profit needed (VND619bn) is about 14% below 3Q26, while 9M26 volume is already 76% of the full-year estimate.
  • Balance sheet, dated: at end-2024 cash and marketable securities were VND14,977bn against debt of VND7,534bn (May 2025 report). The October 2026 update gives no balance-sheet figures.
  • Thin points: net margin is 0.5% in 9M26 (0.8% in 3Q26), 9M26 net profit is only 64% of FY26F, and the expected return to the VND14,300 target is 10%.

What breaks the thesis: Extension of the VND350/liter discount or a cut to the "regulated profit" squeezes margin (about VND90bn per month); or market consolidation ends and volume growth slows. Falling oil prices would also bring inventory write-downs, the main risk in the May 2025 report.
