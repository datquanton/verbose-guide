Banks: milestones, drivers and cushion for the 12 banks Mirae Asset covers

Mirae Asset rates the sector Neutral but 11 of 12 banks Buy or Trading Buy. Profit still grows on credit volume, while funding costs and new bad debt peak only in 1H27; loan-loss coverage is the thinnest part of most banks' cushion.

Key points

  • The profit engine is credit volume. System credit grew 11.44% year to date by mid-October; Mirae Asset forecasts 16.8% for 2026. Growth is two-speed: VPB +28.4% after nine months, CTG, BID and VCB only 7.5-8.5%.
  • The pressure arrives late. The average posted 12-month deposit rate is 5.70% and 3-month interbank sits near 7%. On past lags, new bad debt peaks in 1H27 and NIM is squeezed into 2H27, so 4Q26 funding cost is the first test.
  • Thick earnings cushion, thin reserve cushion. At every bank pre-provision profit far exceeds provisions (VCB, nine months: VND49.6tn against VND9.2tn). But 9 of 12 banks have coverage below 100%, so each extra point of bad debt goes straight to profit.
  • Valuations sit below history. Trailing P/B is mostly 1.0-1.2x; MBB at 1.16x and VPB at 1.09x compare with five-year averages of 1.38x and 1.35x cited by Mirae Asset. Top picks lean to state-owned banks, ACB and MBB.
  • Not every gap means the same. STB, TPB and EIB targets were set in April-June 2026; ACB and VIB targets were restated in flashes that warn of forecast cuts.
How to read: drivers, milestones, cushion

Underlying drivers are the few variables that set a company's earnings (volume, price, margin, funding cost). Milestones are dated, checkable events that show whether the thesis is still on track. Cushion is what limits the fall if the drivers disappoint: valuation, balance sheet, earnings already secured and dividends. The gap to target price is only one part of the cushion, and it means something only while the target is fresh.

1 · Industry: what drives bank earnings now

Sector rating Neutral (Sep 2026). Listed banks' PBT rose 19.6% YoY in 1H26 and most covered banks trade below historical valuation ranges, but deposit rates are expected to stay high through at least 1H27, with new NPL formation peaking in 1H27 and NIM pressure lasting into 2H27. Top picks tilt to state-owned banks, ACB and MBB.

  • Credit growth: system credit rose 10.89% YTD by 28 Sep 2026 and 11.44% by mid-October; Mirae forecasts 16.8% for 2026 and 15% for 2027. Growth is two-speed: VPB +28.4% YTD at 9M26 versus 7.5-8.5% at CTG, BID and VCB.
  • Funding and rates: deposits rose 9.78% YTD by 28 Sep 2026 and loans exceed deposits by about VND2,600tn. The system-average posted 12-month deposit rate is 5.70% (21 Oct) and could hover around 6% in 1H27; the 3-month interbank rate stays near 7%.
  • NIM: sector cost of funds rose 0.6%p to 4.8% in 2Q26. Rising deposit rates lead NIM by about 4-5 quarters; in the 4Q22-1Q23 analogue NIM fell about 50bps over four quarters. Pressure is expected to persist into 2H27.
  • Asset quality and provisioning: rising deposit rates lead new NPL formation by about 2-3 quarters, with a peak expected in 1H27. Loan-loss coverage is only about 79% (106-143% at the start of the last cycle); the NPL ratio of listed banks was 2.01% in 2Q26.
  • Capital: CAR was broadly stable in 2Q26 but softened at fast growers such as VPB. Planned raises in company reports: VPB private placement of about 624mn shares, BID issuance of about 10.7%, VCB private placement of about 6.5%.

2 · Cushion: price against target, and what sits behind it

For a bank, the gap to target is cushion only if the provisioning forecast holds. The real cushion sits in three places: pre-provision profit against provisions, loan-loss coverage, and capital. VCB, CTG and TCB are thick on all three. MBB has the largest gap among recent targets (+62%) but coverage of 84.7% and CAR forecast to fall to 9.9%. HDB, VIB, EIB and STB have coverage below or around 50%, so their risk sits in provisions, not margins.

VCBJoint Stock Commercial Bank for Foreign Trade of VietnamBuy

Target price 69,300 (2 Nov 2026) · price 50,400 · gap to target +38% · 9M26 profit 75.6% · Coverage ≥100% · P/E 10.1x · P/B 1.66x

Core income is strong (3Q26 NII +39.1% YoY, TTM NIM 2.94%) but 3Q26 PBT was flat YoY as provisions jumped to VND6,184bn, mainly for corporate bonds. Coverage of 243.2% and CASA of 32.2% remain among the strongest in the system.

* The VND69,300 target dates from a 7 Aug 2026 report not among those read here, where the rating history table lists it as Trading Buy. The 2 Nov 2026 results note prints Buy (expected return +29.3%) without stating an upgrade.

Underlying drivers

  • NIM and NII: TTM NIM widened to 2.94% in 3Q26 (+13bps QoQ, +28bps YoY) against a 2.76% FY26F; 9M26 NII rose 34.5% YoY and is 80.8% of the full-year forecast.
  • Credit and funding: loans +8.5% YTD (+3.3% QoQ) to VND1,820tn; deposits lag at +6.5% YTD. Estimated bank-only LDR eased to 78.5%.
  • Provisions: 3Q26 provisions were VND6,184bn (3Q25: VND776bn), of which VND5.0tn for corporate bonds; 9M26 provisions of VND9,180bn already exceed the full-year forecast of VND6.3tn, which may be revised up.
  • Asset quality: NPL ratio rose 10bps QoQ to 0.71% (+13bps YTD) and the broader ratio including Group 2 loans to 0.98%; the FY26F NPL forecast is 0.84%.

Milestones

  • 4Q26 9M26 PBT of VND40,440bn (+22.1% YoY) is 75.6% of the VND53.5tn full-year forecast; Mirae may raise full-year provisioning in its next update.
  • 2026 (timing subject to market conditions) Private placement of about 6.5%: the April 2026 report expected capital raising to be brought forward to 2026 as management indicated the need to strengthen CAR.
  • FY26 Year-end forecasts: loan-loss coverage 173.3% and NPL ratio 0.84%, against 243.2% and 0.71% at 3Q26.
  • TBD (not in forecasts) Possible reversal of bond provisions: at a preliminary recovery rate of about 56%, reversals could reach about VND3.6tn if the exposures are recovered or restructured.

Cushion

  • Loan-loss coverage 243.2% at 3Q26 (end-2Q26: 279.2%), with reserves of VND31.2tn against NPLs of VND12.8tn. Bond provisions of VND6.35tn cover 97.7% of HTM corporate bond holdings.
  • Funding: CASA of 32.2% is among the highest in the sector and the estimated bank-only LDR is 78.5%.
  • Earnings buffer: 9M26 pre-provision profit of VND49,620bn (+39.9% YoY) against 9M26 provisions of VND9,180bn.
  • Capital is the thinner spot: reported total CAR of 11.7% for FY25 (FY24: 12.2%). On valuation, the April 2026 target implied about 2.21x P/B, close to -1 standard deviation from the long-term average.

What breaks the thesis: Faster NPL formation as lending rates rise (3Q26: reserves +3.9% against NPLs +19.4%), further bond provisions without recoveries, or tighter capital headroom if the private placement slips.

BIDJoint Stock Commercial Bank for Investment and Development of VietnamBuy

3Q26 PBT rose 39.3% YoY mainly because provisions fell 25.0%, while NII slowed and quarterly NIM narrowed. Coverage is down to 73.4%. The target implies about 1.5x 2026F P/B, below the historical average of about 2.0x.

* The 2 Nov 2026 results note prints Buy (expected return +32.3%) without stating an upgrade; the 30 Sep 2026 report that set the VND42,350 target rated the stock Trading Buy at a share price of VND35,950.

Underlying drivers

  • NIM: trailing 12-month NIM 2.22% (+2bps QoQ), but quarterly NIM fell 11bps and 3Q26 NII fell 4.2% QoQ. The September report cut the FY26F NIM forecast to 2.34% from 2.48%.
  • Credit and funding: loans +8.0% YTD (+2.4% QoQ) against a 13.5% FY26F assumption; deposits +6.2% YTD. Estimated LDR of 81.6% is approaching the 85% cap, so 4Q credit growth hinges on deposit mobilisation.
  • Provisions: 3Q26 provisions fell 25.0% YoY to VND4,640bn and 9M26 provisions fell 5.4%. With NPL ratio at 1.85% (+38bps YTD) and Group 2 loans at 1.54% (+32bps QoQ), Mirae expects higher provisioning ahead.
  • Recoveries of written-off loans: estimated about VND3.0tn in 3Q26 (+19.5% YoY) and VND8.3tn in 9M26 (+14.1%); the 4Q25 base of about VND7.5tn may cap 4Q26 upside.

Milestones

  • 4Q26 4Q26 PBT needs to reach about VND10.7tn (-24.7% YoY off a high 4Q25 base) to meet the VND40.2tn full-year forecast; 9M26 PBT is at 73.4%.
  • 4Q26 Loan growth must accelerate from +8.0% YTD toward the 13.5% FY26F assumption with the LDR at 81.6% against the 85% cap.
  • FY26 Year-end forecasts (September report): coverage 82.2% and NPL ratio 1.74%, against 73.4% and 1.85% at 3Q26; Mirae expects higher provisioning for the rest of the year.
  • TBD (timing not stated) A further share issuance of about 10.7% is planned to support CAR; Mirae expects future capital raising to be incremental, not the re-rating catalyst of earlier cycles.

Cushion

  • Loan-loss coverage is thin: 73.4% at 3Q26, down from 75.9% at end-2Q26 and 99.9% at end-2025; the broad NPL ratio (Groups 2-5) is 3.40%.
  • Capital is thin: reported total CAR of 9.2% for FY25 (FY24: 9.0%); State ownership well above the minimum threshold leaves room for further equity issuance.
  • Earnings buffer: 9M26 pre-provision profit of VND44,914bn (+12.0% YoY); in 3Q26 pre-provision profit was VND15,054bn against provisions of VND4,640bn.
  • Valuation: the target implies about 1.5x 2026F P/B, against a trailing P/B of about 1.33x in the September report and a historical average of about 2.0x. CASA is 20.1%.

What breaks the thesis: Deteriorating asset quality with a thinning provision buffer (Group 2 loans +32bps QoQ to 1.54%, coverage 73.4%) forcing higher provisions, and a slower-than-expected NIM recovery as the LDR nears its cap.
