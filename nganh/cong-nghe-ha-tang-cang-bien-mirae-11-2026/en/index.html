Technology, telecom infrastructure and ports: milestones, drivers and cushion

FPT trades at 8.6 times trailing earnings, below its five-year −2 standard deviation, after Mirae Asset raised its discount rate for AI-driven pricing risk. CTR grows on new construction and IT-solution contracts; GMD's price has reached its target. All three hold more cash than debt.

Key points

  • FPT: the valuation already prices the AI worry. Global IT revenue rose 11.6% over nine months, but 3Q26 signed revenue rose only 3.6%. Mirae Asset added 0.58 percentage points to the discount rate (WACC 11.4%) for pricing risk, while FY26 profit forecasts barely moved. Nearest milestone: 4Q26 needs revenue +17.8%, with Japan +20%.
  • CTR: new contracts offset thin margins. Nine-month revenue rose 23% on construction and IT and solar solutions, but thin construction margins hold FY26 forecast net profit growth to +3%. Viettel-related entities provided 55% of 1Q26 revenue.
  • GMD: profit is past forecast on one-off gains. Nine-month pre-tax profit equals 105.4% of the full-year forecast, but 2027 faces a high base; the gap to target is zero and Mirae Asset may raise the required return.
  • Targets fell partly because share counts rose. FPT's bonus shares, CTR's stock dividend and GMD's rise from 426mn to 649mn shares cut per-share targets without changing value; the note under each ticker says how much is valuation.

How to read this. Underlying drivers are the few variables that set a company's earnings (volume, price, margin, funding cost). Milestones are dated, checkable events that show whether the thesis is still on track. Cushion is what limits the fall if the drivers disappoint: valuation, balance sheet, earnings already secured and dividends. The gap to target price is only one part of the cushion, and it means something only while the target is fresh.

1 · Industry: what drives earnings now

Technology, telecom infrastructure and ports

Seaports: Neutral maintained (Aug 2026): manufacturing shifts and China trade support volumes, while inflation and geopolitics pressure export demand. No sector rating is given for IT or telecom infrastructure; FPT and CTR are rated Buy on growth at reduced targets, GMD is Hold with +3.6% expected return.

  • Global IT services demand: FPT's Global IT revenue grew 11.6% YoY in 9M26, but signed revenue rose only 3.6% YoY in 3Q26 after +32.3% in 1H26; in 3Q26 Japan slowed to +8.3%, the US grew 8.0% and APAC fell 1.6%.
  • AI shift: digital transformation was 50.8% of FPT's Global IT revenue in 1H26 (+23.8% YoY), AI and data analytics grew 54%, traditional work only 4.3%. Mirae added 0.58pp to FPT's discount rate for AI-driven pricing pressure.
  • Port volumes: Vietnam's 7M26 container throughput rose 8% to 20.7mn TEUs and total clearance volume 19% YoY to 797.5mn tons; import-export value reached US$659.5bn (+28.1% YoY), with a 7M26 trade deficit of US$20.5bn.
  • Trade policy and energy: the US applied 12.5% Section 301 tariffs to Vietnamese goods from 24 July 2026; the Strait of Hormuz remains mostly closed, keeping oil, shipping and charter rates high. VEFTA's impact is positive but modest.
  • Common threads: higher discount rates lowered or threaten targets (FPT WACC 11.4%, CTR higher risk-free rate, GMD may lift its 13% required return), and share issues (FPT bonus shares, CTR stock dividend, GMD 426mn to 649mn shares) reset per-share targets.

2 · Cushion: price against target, and what sits behind it

FPT has cushion on both sides: a +63% gap to its reduced target, net cash (Mirae Asset adds VND16.2tn to its DCF) and a P/E below any level of the past five years. The weak spot is that 86.7% of DCF value is terminal value, so it rests on long-term growth. CTR has a +44% gap but a P/B of 3.4x, so its cushion is net cash and contracts, not valuation. GMD has no gap left.

3 · Company by company

FPTFPT CorporationBuy

Target price 76,363 (2 Nov 2026) · price 46,950 · gap to target +63% · 9M26 profit ≈72% · Net cash · P/E 8.6x · P/B 2.06x

Buy maintained. 9M26 NPATMI rose 13.3% YoY and FY26F is broadly unchanged, with Domestic IT offsetting a slower Japan. The target was cut through a higher discount rate for AI-driven pricing risk; trailing P/E of 10.0x is below the 5-year -2SD.

* Targets are not comparable across reports. Aug to Nov 2026: bonus shares raised shares outstanding from 1,714mn to 1,886mn; Mirae restates the prior VND89,154 as VND81,049 (bonus-adjusted), so the like-for-like cut to VND76,363 comes from a 0.58pp higher discount rate (WACC 11.4%) for AI-driven pricing risk, with FY26F NPATMI adjusted by only -0.6%. Mar to Aug 2026 (VND98,730 to VND89,154): printed shares outstanding did not rise (1,767.8mn to 1,714mn) and FY26F NPATMI was nearly unchanged (VND10,796bn to VND10,842bn), while the P/E implied by the target fell from 15.6x to 14.1x FY26F, so that cut was a lower valuation; the August report gives no explicit reason. Printed share prices: 78,800 (10 Mar), 72,600 (26 Aug), 50,200 (2 Nov, after the bonus issue).

Underlying drivers

  • Global IT (51.4% of 9M26 PBT): 9M26 revenue +11.6% YoY, 3Q26 +8.2%. Japan, 46% of segment revenue, slowed to +8.3% YoY in 3Q26 from +24.8% in 2Q26. FY26F assumes Global IT +12.2% and Japan +17.8%.
  • Order intake: Global IT signed revenue rose 3.6% YoY to VND9,799bn in 3Q26 after +32.3% in 1H26; 9M26 book-to-bill is 1.27x (9M25: 1.15x). Mirae trimmed FY27-28F revenue growth to +14.5% and +15.0%.
  • Domestic IT: revenue +43.0% YoY in 3Q26 and +29.8% in 9M26; 9M26 PBT margin 7.8% (9M25: 4.8%), half of Global IT's 15.8%. FY26F growth assumption raised to +30% from +15%.
  • Associates and the yen: Education, Investment & Others (mostly equity-method income; FPT Telecom is 45.66% owned) supplied 44% of the VND1,274bn rise in 9M26 PBT. Each 5% JPY/VND move shifts FY27F Japan revenue by about VND1.1tn.

Milestones

  • passed: 27 Oct 2026 Ransomware group The Gentlemen listed FPT Software on its leak site; the claim is unverified and Mirae sees no basis to quantify an earnings impact. A confirmed compromise of customer information would pose reputational and commercial risks.
  • 4Q26 To reach FY26F, 4Q26 needs revenue of VND17,681bn (+17.8% YoY; 3Q26: +12.1%) and NPATMI of VND3,001bn (+15.6%; 3Q26: +11.8%), including Japan revenue of VND5,007bn (+20%).
  • FY26 9M26 PBT was VND8,770bn, against the company's FY26 plan of VND11,629bn (revenue plan VND58,580bn) and Mirae's FY26F PBT of VND12,177bn.
  • FY26 AI factories earned over US$12mn at roughly 90% utilisation in 1H26, against full-year guidance of US$25-30mn; their profit remains immaterial at group level.
  • FY27 FY27F: revenue VND66,071bn (+14.5%) and NPATMI VND12,331bn. Mirae calls the yen the biggest swing factor: every 5% move in JPY/VND shifts group PBT by about 1.2%.

Cushion

  • Valuation: trailing P/E of 10.0x on 29 Oct 2026 is below the 5-year -2SD (10.7x) and 49% under the 5-year average (19.8x). The target price implies 13.4x FY26F P/E.
  • Balance sheet: the DCF adds net cash of VND16,153bn; net debt to equity is forecast at -39.7% (net cash) and interest coverage at 14.2x for FY26F.
  • Earnings buffer: 9M26 Global IT signed revenue of VND36,137bn with book-to-bill at 1.27x; FY26F ROE 27.3%, DPS VND2,165 and payout ratio 37.9%.
  • Thin spots: terminal value is 86.7% of the DCF enterprise value, and Global IT added only 34% of the 9M26 PBT increase (margin 15.8% vs. 15.9%), with associates doing most of the lifting.

What breaks the thesis: AI-driven pricing pressure on IT services eroding Global IT pricing and margins; order intake staying weak after 3Q26 (+3.6%) with Japan slowing further or the yen weakening; a confirmed data compromise at FPT Software hurting relations with international outsourcing clients.

CTRViettel Construction Joint Stock CorporationBuy

Target price 86,200 (30 Oct 2026) · price 59,800 · gap to target +44% · 9M26 profit 82% · Net cash · P/E 11.6x · P/B 3.44x

Buy maintained in the 3Q26 flash (upgraded from Trading Buy in Aug 2026). New contracts in construction and IT/solar solutions drive revenue (9M26 +23% YoY), but thinner construction margins hold NPAT growth to +16%; FY26F NPAT is VND620bn (+3% YoY).

* The cut from VND98,800 (19 May 2026) to VND86,200 (11 Aug 2026) is mostly a share-count effect: shares outstanding rose from 114mn to 128mn after a stock dividend, and the target fell in roughly the same proportion; Mirae also raised the risk-free rate (10-year government bond yield). In the same report FY26F NPAT was set at VND620bn, below the VND674bn of the May report, and the rating was upgraded to Buy from Trading Buy. The 30 Oct 2026 flash repeats the target and rating without a new valuation.

Underlying drivers

  • Construction (35% of 3Q26 revenue): 3Q26 revenue VND1,490bn (+38% YoY); 9M26 new contract value VND4,595bn (+76% YoY). FY26F growth assumption raised to 30% from 14%.
  • Margins: construction gross margin fell to 3.6% in 3Q26 (3Q25: 4.8%) as raw material costs rose 73% YoY. Mirae forecasts an operating margin of 4.5% in FY26F (FY25: 5.3%) and 4.3% in FY27F.
  • Tower leasing: 12,000 BTS stations, co-located sites up to 648 (2Q26: 573); 3Q26 revenue VND246bn (+11% YoY), gross margin 30.2% (3Q25: 28.8%). FY26F assumptions: growth 15%, gross margin 27%.
  • IT integrated solutions and network operation: IT solutions revenue VND808bn in 3Q26 (+28% YoY) on solar execution, FY26F +30%; network operation VND1,683bn (-17% YoY, high base), FY26F +3% on signs of market saturation.

Milestones

  • passed: Aug 2026 Rating upgraded to Buy from Trading Buy, with the target lowered to VND86,200 from VND98,800 after a stock dividend (shares outstanding 114mn to 128mn) and a higher risk-free rate.
  • 4Q26 9M26 revenue (VND12,186bn) and NPAT (VND505bn) reached 75% and 82% of Mirae's 2026 forecast (VND16,272bn and VND620bn). Mirae tends to maintain FY26F, pending further evaluation.
  • 4Q26 Co-located leased sites rose from 494 (1Q26) to 573 (2Q26) and 648 (3Q26), with the tower count unchanged at 12,000. The next quarterly count shows whether tenancy keeps rising.
  • FY26 Company targets for FY26: revenue VND15,653bn (+12%), pre-tax profit VND778bn (+4%) and NPAT VND622bn (+4%). 9M26 pre-tax profit is VND630bn.
  • FY27 FY27F: revenue VND19,232bn and NPAT VND732bn, with the operating margin at 4.3%.

Cushion

  • Balance sheet is net cash: end-2025 cash VND1,705bn plus short-term investments VND1,618bn, against borrowings of VND1,760bn (short-term) and VND503bn (long-term); net interest-bearing debt/EBITDA -0.98x in FY25, -2.37x in FY26F.
  • Earnings buffer: Viettel-related entities provided 55% of 1Q26 revenue (VND2,095bn); 9M26 new contracts of VND4,595bn in construction and about VND1,699bn in IT solutions back the coming quarters' revenue.
  • Returns: FY26F ROE of 27.7% and a dividend yield of about 2%.
  • Thin spots: FY26F operating margin is only 4.5%; P/E on the target price is 17.8x FY26F against a market P/E of 12.9x, and P/B is 4.6x. 9M26 G&A rose 48% and financial expenses 59% YoY.

What breaks the thesis: Raw material costs keep compressing construction margins while construction's revenue share grows; network operation saturates and leasing growth slows further; provisions rise with construction volume (1H26 provisioning expenses up 6.6x YoY to VND36.5bn).

GMDGemadept CorporationHold

Target price 52,500 (2 Nov 2026) · price 52,500 · gap to target +0%† · 9M26 profit 105.4% · Net cash · P/E 12.4x · P/B 2.37x

Hold maintained. 9M26 results track full-year forecasts and after-tax profit already exceeds FY26F thanks to one-off investment transfer gains, but margins are narrowing, 2027 faces a high base and the expected return is +3.6%.

* Not a valuation cut. Shares outstanding rose from 426mn (29 May 2026 report) to 649mn (2 Nov 2026 report), about 1.5 times, and the target moved from VND78,800 to VND52,500 in the same proportion. The November report's rating history restates the 29 May target as VND52,500 and the 12 Mar target as VND48,300, and the 52-week high is restated from 89,500 to 59,667. The reports do not name the corporate action. FY26F NPATMI stays at VND1,890bn. The underlying valuation dates from 29 May 2026 (about six months old), and Mirae says it may raise the required rate of return, which would lower the valuation.

Underlying drivers

  • Container volume: 1Q26 volume through GMD's system reached 1.2mn TEU (+8.4% YoY); south +15.7% to 905,000 TEU, north -7.9% despite Nam Dinh Vu Phase 3. Mirae's 2026 assumption: 4.48mn TEUs excluding Gemalink (2025 estimate: 3.68mn).
  • Revenue and pricing: 3Q26 revenue +41.6% YoY to VND2,209.8bn; 9M26 VND5,423.7bn (+25.2%), 75.7% of FY26F VND7,164.9bn. The forecast assumes service prices stay steady, so growth is volume-led.
  • Costs and margin: 3Q26 cost of goods sold rose 68.6% YoY and gross margin fell 10.9 percentage points to 31.8%; 9M26 gross margin was 41.7% (-3.3 points) against FY26F 39.1%. 9M26 selling expenses +59.4% YoY.
  • Associates (Gemalink, SCS): 9M26 profit from associates VND1,043.4bn (+36% YoY); Gemalink contributed VND281.2bn in 3Q26 (+35.1% YoY). FY26F profit from associates: VND1,342.1bn (+15.9% YoY).

Milestones

  • passed: Oct 2025 Nam Dinh Vu Phase 3 officially started operating, adding an estimated 150,000 TEUs to northern volume in 4Q25; northern volume still fell 7.9% YoY in 1Q26.
  • 4Q26 9M26 after-tax profit of VND2,524.9bn is already 101.5% of FY26F (VND2,487bn), while revenue is at 75.7%; Mirae may raise forecasts for some indicators because of the surge in financial income.
  • FY26 Company guidance for 2026: revenue VND6,500bn and pre-tax profit VND2,800bn; 9M26 pre-tax profit is VND2,977.4bn. GMD's actual pre-tax profit exceeded guidance in every year from 2021 to 2025.
  • 2027 High base: 2026 includes one-off gains on transferring long-term investments (9M26 financial revenue VND837bn vs. VND141bn in 9M25), which pressures 2027 growth. Mirae's FY27F NPATMI is VND2,183bn (May 2026 forecast).

Cushion

  • Balance sheet: cash and equivalents of VND4,360.9bn at end-2025, against short-term debt of VND307.5bn and long-term debt of VND2,081.6bn; total debt to equity 16.1% (FY26F: 15.2%) and interest coverage 15.8x in FY26F.
  • Earnings buffer: profit from associates (Gemalink, SCS) was VND1,043.4bn in 9M26, and 9M26 pre-tax profit already equals 105.4% of Mirae's FY26F.
  • Dividend: FY26F dividend yield of 3.0% in the May 2026 forecast (FY25: 2.8%).
  • Valuation cushion is thin: FY26F P/E of 19.6x against a market P/E of 12.9x, an expected return of only +3.6% at the VND52,500 target, and part of 2026 profit is one-off.

What breaks the thesis: Weaker global trade from tariffs or the US-Iran conflict cutting volumes; capacity expansion and local competition around Nam Dinh Vu and Lach Huyen (including Vingroup's Nam Do Son port project); a higher required rate of return in Mirae's model lowering the valuation.
