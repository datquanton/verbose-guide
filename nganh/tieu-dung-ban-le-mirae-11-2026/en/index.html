Consumer staples and retail: milestones, drivers and cushion

Staples grow on price and product mix rather than volume, but carry the thickest balance sheets (VNM, SAB and QNS all hold more cash than debt). Retail profit is beating forecasts, while FRT's price is already close to its target.

Key points

  • Staples growth comes from price and mix. QNS management says fast-moving consumer goods grew only modestly with no clear volume pickup, and selling costs are rising faster than sales (QNS 3Q26 +35.4%, SAB nine months +16.1%). Even so, nine-month profit tracks forecasts: VNM 87.9%, SAB 83.1%.
  • The staples cushion is cash and dividends. VNM holds VND23.1tn of cash and equivalents against VND9.4tn of short-term debt; SAB is almost debt-free and pays a VND5,000 dividend per share for 2026 (current price VND44,000); QNS holds VND5.9tn of net cash.
  • Retail grows from existing stores. Same-store sales in MWG's phone and electronics chains rose about 30% over nine months; Bach Hoa Xanh added 526 stores in 3Q26 and stayed profitable; Long Chau holds 11% of the pharmacy market.
  • Nearest milestones: the year-end season against the high iPhone 17 base of 2025, DMX's VND2,000 dividend per share in December 2026, and sugar inventories expected to fall to 285k tons by year-end, supporting sugar prices for QNS.

How to read this. Underlying drivers are the few variables that set a company's earnings (volume, price, margin, funding cost). Milestones are dated, checkable events that show whether the thesis is still on track. Cushion is what limits the fall if the drivers disappoint: valuation, balance sheet, earnings already secured and dividends. The gap to target price is only one part of the cushion, and it means something only while the target is fresh.

1 · Industry: what drives earnings now

Consumer staples (dairy, beer, sugar and soy milk)

No sector rating is printed; Mirae rates VNM Buy, SAB and QNS Trading Buy. 9M26 profits track its FY26 forecasts (NPAT at 87.9% of FY26F for VNM, 83.1% for SAB), but growth comes from price and product mix while selling costs rise, and 3Q26 showed signs of slowing, so VNM and SAB forecasts were left unchanged.

  • Domestic demand: retail sales rose 13.1% YoY in 7M26 (July: +14.5%), yet QNS management said FMCG consumption grew only modestly in 9M26 with no meaningful volume pickup, and beer demand stays weak on living costs and sobriety checks.
  • Input costs: milk powder stood at US$3,448/ton (SMP) and US$3,666/ton (WMP) at end-April 2026, and Mirae raised its 2026 assumption to US$3,000/MT from US$2,600; brewers face higher imported-material, shipping and fuel costs with oil near US$90.
  • Sugar: domestic prices rose 22-24% since August 2026 to VND19,000-20,000/kg as inventories fall, although the world market is still expected to post an 8.0mn-ton surplus in 2026; Mirae builds in only a modest margin improvement.
  • Growth relies on price and mix, funded by higher selling spend: QNS 3Q26 SG&A +35.4% YoY (21.8% of sales), SAB 9M26 selling expenses +16.1% YoY, and VNM's revenue growth stems from price increases and portfolio expansion begun in 2Q25.
  • Trade (VEFTA): Vietnam cuts import duties over a 3-to-10-year roadmap (dairy 5-10 years, beer and wine mostly 10 years). EFTA supplied US$24.5mn of dairy imports in 2025 (1.7% of the total): modest pressure on VNM, negligible for SAB.

Modern retail (phones, electronics, grocery, pharmacy)

No formal sector rating in these reports. Mirae sees a long runway for modern retail but expects growth to become less forgiving as competition rises; data show no material slowdown in discretionary demand. Stock calls: MWG Buy (TP VND100,000), FRT Hold (TP VND159,500) after its share price rose to VND148,700.

  • Phones and electronics: growth comes from existing stores, not new ones. MWG's ICT-CE same-store sales rose about 30% in 9M26 on a flat store base; FPT Shop's monthly sales per store rose 27.5% YoY to VND3.02bn in 3Q26 with 622 stores.
  • Price and mix: chip and memory shortages lifted device prices in 2026 (Apple raised selected devices and laptops 20-30% in July 2026). Euromonitor sees 8.8% CAGR for selected ICT-CE categories over 2026-30, partly price inflation; BNPL reached 38% of DMX sales.
  • Grocery roll-out: BHX had 3,191 stores at end-1H26 (+46% YoY) and added 526 in 3Q26; 3Q26 revenue rose 48.9% YoY to VND17.6tn with gross margin 24.1%, but expansion costs held operating margin near 2.3%.
  • Pharmacy: organised chains hold about 15% of a VND290tn pharmacy retail market (2025) that Euromonitor projects to grow 5.2% a year to 2030. Long Chau has 11% share, 2,741 pharmacies at end-September 2026 and a 3Q26 operating margin of 4.9%.
  • Operating leverage: SG&A grows slower than sales as chains scale. 3Q26 operating margin was 6.7% at MWG (3Q25: 4.5%) and 4.0% at FRT (+1.4%p YoY); FRT's SG&A/sales fell to 15.9% from 16.8%.

VNMViet Nam Dairy Products JSCBuy

Target price 86,800 (2 Nov 2026) · price 53,000 · gap to target +64% · 9M26 profit 87.9% · Net cash · P/E 9.6x · P/B 3.17x

Buy kept: the leading dairy company, with the largest domestic share in liquid and formula milk, returned to growth after rebranding (9M26 revenue +14.4% YoY, NPAT +30.6% YoY). Forecasts and the VND86,800 FCFF target are unchanged pending 4Q26.

* The VND86,800 target was set on 4 Aug 2026 and kept in the 2 Nov 2026 results update. The May 2026 report printed VND76,800; later reports restate that target as VND75,000 after a dividend adjustment, so the two are not directly comparable.

Underlying drivers

  • Domestic sales: 3Q26 net revenue +8.8% YoY to VND14,718bn (1Q26 +20.4%, 2Q26 +6.5%), driven by price increases and portfolio expansion since 2Q25. Mirae expected 2H26 growth to slow; FY26F total revenue VND68,998bn (+8.3% YoY).
  • Overseas sales: 3Q26 net revenue +8.3% YoY to VND3,747bn, slowing from +39.2% YoY in 2Q26; subsidiary Angkor Milk grew 42.4% YoY. Mirae flags global uncertainties as a risk to overseas growth.
  • Gross margin and milk powder: 3Q26 gross margin 41.3% (-0.4pp YoY), 9M26 42.5% (+1.3pp). FY26F assumes 39.2%, with milk powder at US$3,000/MT (raised from US$2,600).
  • Financial items: 3Q26 financial income +51.7% YoY to VND600.3bn (interest income +25.5% to VND441.8bn), financial expenses +65.8% YoY to VND151.4bn. Mirae raised both FY26F lines on higher interest rates.

Milestones

  • passed: Aug 2026 Target price raised to VND86,800 from VND75,000 (dividend adjusted) and FY26F revenue raised to VND68,998bn from VND66,193.3bn, after 1H26 NPAT rose 38.4% YoY.
  • 4Q26 9M26 NPAT of VND8,604.8bn is 87.9% of Mirae's FY26F VND9,787.7bn (NPAT including minorities), leaving about VND1,183bn for 4Q26; 9M26 revenue is 77.5% of FY26F VND68,998bn. Mirae monitors 4Q26 growth indicators.
  • FY26 Company 2026 guidelines: revenue VND66,477bn and NPAT VND9,828bn; 9M26 delivered VND53,506bn and VND8,604.8bn. In 2025 VNM fell 2.8% short of its NPAT guideline.
  • FY27F Mirae forecasts FY27F revenue of VND71,870bn (+4.2% YoY) and NPATMI of VND10,613bn (+9.5% YoY), with operating margin rising to 18.6% from 17.7% in FY26F.

Cushion

  • Balance sheet: cash and equivalents of VND23,149.7bn at end-2025 against short-term debt of VND9,393.7bn and long-term debt of VND62.9bn; total debt to equity 27.4% (FY26F 25.9%), FY26F interest coverage 20.2x.
  • Dividend: Mirae assumes a cash dividend of VND4,000 per share in 2026 (2025: VND5,350); its table prints a 6.6% yield for 2026-2028.
  • Earnings buffer: 9M26 NPAT already equals 87.9% of Mirae's FY26F, and the 9M26 gross margin of 42.5% is above the 39.2% assumed for the full year.
  • Valuation: the share price of VND56,000 (2 Nov 2026) was near the 52-week low of VND54,600 (high VND75,500). The printed 20.8x FY26F P/E is at the target price (market P/E 12.9x); no comparison with VNM's own history is given.

What breaks the thesis: Increasing domestic competition (including premium dairy imports as VEFTA duties phase out), higher input prices such as milk powder, changes in M&A or investment plans, and Middle East uncertainty hitting exports. Mirae may also raise its required return, which would lower the valuation.

SABSaigon Beer - Alcohol - Beverage CorporationTrading Buy

Target price 48,500 (2 Nov 2026) · price 44,000 · gap to target +10% · 9M26 profit 83.1% · Net cash · P/E 11.9x · P/B 2.66x

Trading Buy kept after 3Q26: 9M26 NPAT (+8.9% YoY) tracks the FY26 forecast, but 3Q26 NPAT fell 9.4% YoY as selling expenses rose. Mirae forecasts flat to lower earnings and values SAB on peers' average P/E.

* The VND48,500 target and the upgrade to Trading Buy (from Hold) date from 31 Jul 2026; the 2 Nov 2026 update kept both. The April 2026 target of VND52,800 was restated as VND49,800 after the 2026 dividend adjustment, so it is not directly comparable.

Underlying drivers

  • Revenue: 3Q26 +4.1% YoY to VND6,778.7bn, 9M26 +5.5% YoY to VND20,331.5bn. Mirae keeps FY26F at VND26,511.5bn (+1% YoY) and assumes a 1.0% yearly selling-price increase, citing weak domestic beer consumption.
  • Gross margin: 3Q26 37.6% (+0.9pp YoY), 9M26 37.5% (+2.7pp). Mirae assumes 35% for FY26F (2025: 35.4%), expecting higher imported-material, shipping and fuel costs to push up COGS.
  • Selling expenses: 3Q26 +9.8% YoY to VND1,077.4bn, 9M26 +16.1% YoY to VND3,131.9bn, mainly advertising and marketing. Mirae's model assumes selling costs at 15.0% of revenue.
  • Financial income: 9M26 +9.1% YoY to VND818.5bn (3Q26 +16.2% YoY to VND292.3bn). Mirae forecasts VND1,140.1bn of interest from financial activities in FY26F against VND1,006.6bn in 2025.

Milestones

  • passed: Jul 2026 Upgraded to Trading Buy from Hold on 31 Jul 2026 after the share price fell 4.2% since the prior update; target lowered to VND48,500 from VND49,800 (2026 dividend adjustment), equivalent to a 15.6x P/E.
  • 4Q26 9M26 NPAT of VND3,760.6bn equals 83.1% of FY26F VND4,525.1bn (NPAT including minorities), leaving about VND764.5bn for 4Q26. Mirae sees a high probability of meeting its 2026 forecast.
  • FY26 Full-year check: 9M26 operating profit is 82.0% of FY26F VND5,656.3bn and gross margin 37.5% versus 35.0% assumed. Mirae flags the YoY weakening in 3Q26 (operating profit -0.3%, NPAT -9.4%) for close monitoring.
  • FY27F Mirae forecasts FY27F revenue of VND26,777bn (+1.0% YoY), operating profit of VND5,555bn (-1.8% YoY) and NPATMI of VND4,289bn (EPS growth -1.8%).
  • 2026-2028 Dividends: SAB announced a cash dividend of VND5,000 per share this year; Mirae models VND5,000 again for 2027 and VND3,500 for 2028.

Cushion

  • Balance sheet: cash and equivalents of VND19,002.4bn at end-2025 (FY26F VND16,764.7bn) against short-term debt of VND267.6bn and long-term debt of VND167.9bn; total debt to equity 1.9%.
  • Dividend: cash dividend of VND5,000 per share for 2026 against a share price of VND41,700 on 2 Nov 2026; Mirae's table prints a 9.5% yield for 2026 and 2027.
  • Valuation: the target applies peers' average P/E of 15.6x, while SAB's own current P/E was 12.9x in the same table (Jul 2026). No comparison with SAB's own history is given.
  • Thin spots: Mirae forecasts NPATMI slipping from VND4,367bn in FY26F to VND4,272bn in FY28F, and dividends above earnings cut forecast equity from VND22,999.8bn (2025) to VND18,883.7bn (2027F).

What breaks the thesis: Weak beer demand under Decree 100/2019/ND-CP combined with rising inflation, higher input prices, or tougher competition would undercut already flat forecasts. A fall in peers' average P/E would lower the target, and SCIC's divestment plan is a listed risk.

QNSQuang Ngai Sugar JSCTrading Buy

Target price 58,400 (5 Nov 2026) · price 47,600 · gap to target +23% · 9M26 profit 93% · Net cash · P/E 8.7x · P/B 1.62x

Trading Buy kept, target raised to VND58,400: premium soymilk drives growth (3Q26 soymilk revenue +23.9% YoY, gross margin 48.3%) and recovering sugar prices offer a nearer catalyst, but higher SG&A keeps FY26F-27F net profit roughly flat.

Underlying drivers

  • Soymilk: 3Q26 revenue +23.9% YoY to VND1,634bn (volume +16%, ASP +6%), gross margin 48.3% (+3.5pp YoY), 72.6% of group gross profit. Mirae forecasts soymilk revenue of VND5,973bn in FY26F and VND6,243bn in FY27F.
  • Sugar: 3Q26 revenue -8.0% YoY to VND871bn, gross margin 21.9% (+1.8pp YoY). Domestic prices have recovered 24% since August 2026 to VND20,000/kg; Mirae factors in only a modest margin improvement for 4Q26 and 2027.
  • SG&A: 3Q26 +35.4% YoY to 21.8% of sales (promotion +67.3%, commissions +199.5% YoY), absorbing about 82% of incremental gross profit. FY26F SG&A VND2,409bn; operating margin 15.3% in FY26F-27F, with leverage expected from FY28.
  • Net financial income: it lifted 3Q26 pre-tax profit growth to +20.1% YoY against operating profit growth of +10.6%. Mirae forecasts VND329bn for FY26F, up from VND195bn in 2025.

Milestones

  • 4Q26 Mirae's FY26F (revenue VND12,287bn, net profit VND1,920bn) implies 4Q26F revenue of VND3,162bn and net profit of VND513bn. 9M26 already reached 87% of the company's FY26 revenue guidance and 93% of its profit guidance.
  • Nov 2026 An Khe sugar plant: VND1,000bn of the VND1,500bn 2026 capital budget, with completion targeted by Nov 2026 according to the March 2026 report. The 5 Nov 2026 report gives no status update.
  • Dec 2026 Domestic sugar inventory is expected at 285k tons at year-end, down from 521k tons at end-3Q26. A sustained domestic price recovery would be upside to Mirae's sugar margin assumptions.
  • FY27F Mirae forecasts FY27F revenue of VND12,942bn (+5.3% YoY) and net profit of VND1,955bn (+1.8% YoY); capex rises to VND1,417bn from VND1,019bn in FY26F.
  • FY28F Operating leverage is expected to emerge: operating margin of 16.0% (15.3% in FY26F-27F) and net profit of VND2,127bn (EPS growth 8.8%).

Cushion

  • Net cash: VND5,867bn at end-2025 (+15% YoY); net cash-to-equity 0.6x in 2025 and 0.5x in FY26F. Mirae's DCF counts cash of VND5,802bn and investments of VND1,741bn against debt of VND4,158bn.
  • Dividend: Mirae assumes DPS of VND3,000 for FY26F-28F (2025: VND3,396), a 5.1% yield at the target price. The new capex cycle (2026 budget VND1,500bn) could moderate distributions.
  • Earnings mix: in a normal year sugar is under 20% of group gross profit and about half of sugar revenue is consumed internally; Vinasoy holds over 90% of the domestic plant-based milk market.
  • Valuation cushion is thin: the target implies 9.3x FY27F P/E, 15% above the 5-year average of 8.1x, a premium that already assumes stronger earnings growth beyond 2027.

What breaks the thesis: Swings in sugar and soybean prices (the larger risk is a prolonged period of low sugar prices), weaker consumer reception of premium soymilk products, or higher-than-expected spending on new launches that delays the operating leverage expected from FY28.

  • FPT Shop: 3Q26 revenue VND5.6tn (+27.0% YoY), monthly sales per store VND3.02bn, operating profit VND121bn, a fifth straight profitable quarter. Mirae's July view was cautious: about VND2bn per store per month is needed to cover fixed costs.
  • Group cost ratio and funding: SG&A/sales fell to 15.9% (3Q25: 16.8%), lifting operating margin to 4.0%. Short-term borrowings rose to VND14.2tn (+34% YTD) to fund inventory of VND15.6tn; net financial expenses were VND27bn.

Milestones

  • passed: Oct-Nov 2026 Rating moved from Buy (TP VND150,000, 16 July 2026) to Trading Buy (TP VND159,000, 1 October 2026) to Hold (TP VND159,500, 2 November 2026) as the share price rose from VND113,600 to VND148,700.
  • 4Q26 9M26 revenue of VND48.3tn and NP of VND1,069bn equal 79% and 80% of Mirae's FY26 estimates; Mirae flags upside risk. Company 2026 targets are revenue VND59,500bn and PBT VND1,550bn; 1Q-3Q26 PBT was VND472bn, VND566bn and VND686bn.
  • 4Q26 FPT Shop meets its peak-season base: 4Q25 CE and mobile revenue was VND5,258bn and operating profit VND91bn. In July Mirae expected this year's iPhone cycle to be weaker than last year's after device price increases.
  • FY2026 Management guides 400-450 net store openings in 2026 (200 in 1H26; 102 pharmacies in 3Q26); the medium-term target is about 3,000 pharmacies against 2,741 at end-September 2026.
  • 2027 Vaccination (under 5% of Long Chau revenue; management targets about 10% in five years) has reached store-level breakeven and is expected to be profitable at company level by 2027.

Cushion

  • Valuation is thin: expected return is 7.3%; the printed FY26F P/E is 21.2x against 12.0x for the market. In July, Long Chau's implied 10.9x FY26 EV/EBITDA was already above the Asian pharmacy median of 6.7x.
  • Reference value: Creador's April 2025 purchase of 13% of Long Chau implied an equity value of VND41.25tn (21.7x FY26 EV/EBITDA); FRT owns 78.59% of Long Chau and its market cap is VND25,732bn.
  • Earnings buffer: pharmacy customers trade down rather than stop buying; pharmaceuticals earned VND585bn of the VND706bn 3Q26 operating profit, and 9M26 NP is already 80% of Mirae's FY26 estimate with one quarter left.
  • Balance sheet is working-capital heavy: short-term borrowings VND14.2tn, 9M26 operating cash flow negative VND1.7tn (3Q26: positive VND1.25tn). Financial income largely offsets interest; the July forecast put net debt-to-equity at 8.5% for 2026F. No dividend is forecast.

What breaks the thesis: Policy or regulation changes for pharmacy retail, cost overruns from network expansion, or weaker demand for ICT-CE and discretionary healthcare. FPT Shop slips into operating loss if monthly sales per store fall below about VND2bn.
